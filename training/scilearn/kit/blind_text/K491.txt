Hero code: K491
Basic attack (level 1 values; several entries mean alternate forms, units or attack modes): range 5.5, one attack every 0.9 s, 81 damage per attack.

ABILITIES
[Q] basic (cooldown 6 s, cost 35): Shoot a flare dealing 75 (+4% per level) damage to enemies hit, and an additional 105 (+4% per level) damage to enemies in the center. If a Hero is hit by the center, [D]'s passive healing instantly activates.
[W] basic (cooldown 12 s, cost 50): Polymorph a target for 1.5 seconds, Slowing their Movement Speed by 30% and Silencing them. Targets are not able to attack while Polymorphed.
[E] basic (cooldown 10 s, cost 60): Increase target Hero's Movement Speed by 20%, and grant them 30 Spell Armor for 3 seconds, reducing Spell damage taken by 30%.
[R1] heroic (cooldown 9 s, cost 40): Teleport to a nearby ally. When teleporting to a Hero, heal them for 200 (+4% per level).  Stores up to 2 charges.
[R2] heroic (cooldown 50 s, cost 90): After 0.5 seconds, create an expanding nova of wind, dealing 225 (+4% per level) damage and pushing enemies away.
[D] trait (cooldown 75 s): Activate to remove all Stun, Root, Slow, and Silence effects from nearby allied Heroes.  Passive: this hero heals nearby allied Heroes for 110 (+4% per level) every 4 seconds
[Z] mount (cooldown 40 s): After 2 seconds, teleport to an allied Hero and heal them for 25% of their maximum Health.
