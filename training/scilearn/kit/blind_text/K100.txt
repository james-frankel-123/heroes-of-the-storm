Hero code: K100
Basic attack (level 1 values; several entries mean alternate forms, units or attack modes): range 5, one attack every 1.35 s, 83 damage per attack.

ABILITIES
[Q] basic (cooldown 12 s): Create a laser beam at the target point that circles around this hero twice, dealing 135 (+4% per level) damage to enemies hit and Slowing them by 25% for 4 seconds.
[W] basic: Basic Attack speed increased by 150%.  Activate to change to [alt-W], increasing Basic Attack range and damage, and causing Basic Attacks to splash.
[E] basic (cooldown 17 s): Warp to a targeted location, phasing out after 0.5 seconds, and arriving 0.75 seconds later.
[R1] heroic (cooldown 100 s): After 0.5 seconds, Channel a powerful beam that spans across the battleground for 4 seconds, dealing 112 (+4% per level) damage every 0.25 seconds to non-Structure enemies hit.
[R2] heroic (cooldown 90 s): Channel for 1.5 seconds, sweeping a laser in front of this hero that locks onto enemy Heroes. Once Channeling finishes, fire 5 missiles at each locked Hero, dealing 79 (+4% per level) damage each. Deals 50% increased damage to Slowed targets.
[D] trait: this hero has a permanent 760 (+4% per level) Shield which regenerates at 10% per second after not taking damage for 5 seconds.
Alternate form or second unit abilities:
[alt-W] basic: Basic Attacks have 1.25 increased range, deal 25% more damage, and splash to nearby enemies.  Activate to change to [W], increasing Basic Attack speed.
