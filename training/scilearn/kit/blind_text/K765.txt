Hero code: K765
Basic attack (level 1 values; several entries mean alternate forms, units or attack modes): range 1.5, one attack every 0.8 s, 78 damage per attack.

ABILITIES
[Q] basic (cooldown 12 s, cost 45): term A to the target area, dealing 110 (+4% per level) damage to nearby enemies and Slowing them by 25% for 2.5 seconds. It can be reactivated within 5 seconds to teleport this hero to term E and Slow nearby enemies again.
[W] basic (cooldown 9 s, cost 45): Shields this hero for 336 (+4% per level) damage and nearby allied Heroes and Minions for 40% as much for 4 seconds.
[E] basic (cooldown 6 s, cost 45): Rake target area for 150 (+4% per level) damage. Allies moving through the targeted area gain 25% increased Movement Speed for 2 seconds.
[R1] heroic (cooldown 70 s, cost 80): After 0.75 seconds, charge an enemy Hero, dealing 150 (+4% per level) damage and Stunning them for 1.5 seconds. Nearby enemies are knocked away and take 75 (+4% per level) damage.
[R2] heroic (cooldown 90 s, cost 75): After 0.5 seconds create a field of holy energy that makes allied Heroes Invulnerable. Lasts 3 seconds.
[D] trait: Upon dying, become Invulnerable and gain 20% Movement Speed for 4 seconds. Upon expiration, explode, dealing 450 (+4% per level) damage and reducing this hero's death timer by 10% per enemy Hero hit. [D] reduces nearby enemy Heroes' damage by  50% while active and for  3 seconds when it expires.
