Hero code: K118
Basic attack (level 1 values; several entries mean alternate forms, units or attack modes): range 1.25, one attack every 1.2 s, 137 damage per attack.

ABILITIES
[Q] basic (cooldown 8 s, cost 40): Deal 81 (+4% per level) damage to enemies in an area. Heroes hit on the outer edge are Stunned for 0.5 seconds and then Slowed by 40% for 2 seconds.
[W] basic (cooldown 12 s, cost 55): Deal 156 (+4% per level) damage to an enemy and heal for 10% of this hero's missing Health. Healing is increased by 100% against Heroes.
[E] basic (cooldown 16 s, cost 60): Throw a nearby enemy Hero, Minion, or Mercenary to the target location, dealing 91 (+4% per level) damage to enemies near the impact and Slowing them by 30% for 2.5 seconds.
[R1] heroic (cooldown 50 s, cost 40): Silence nearby Heroes and force them to attack this hero for 2 seconds.
[R2] heroic (cooldown 8 s, cost 25): Deal 50 (+4% per level) damage to nearby enemies and Slow them by 40% for 1.5 seconds. Deals 100% more damage to Heroes, and each Hero hit reduces the cooldown by 1 second.  Stores up to 3 charges.
[D] trait: this hero gains 1 Armor for every 2% of maximum Health missing.
[1] activable (cooldown 45 s): Activate to throw a nearby ally and grant them 20 Armor for 3 seconds. Deals 91 (+4% per level) damage to nearby enemies upon impact and Slows them by 30% for 2.5 seconds.  While in flight, allied Heroes are Unstoppable.
