Hero code: K245
Basic attack (level 1 values; several entries mean alternate forms, units or attack modes): range 5.5, one attack every 0.8 s, 60 damage per attack.

ABILITIES
[Q] basic (cooldown 4 s, cost 25): Heal the lowest Health allied Hero within 6 range for 175 (+4% per level) Health.
[W] basic (cooldown 11 s, cost 35): Summon a [W] on an allied Hero that attacks nearby enemies every second. Each attack deals 26 (+4% per level) damage and heals the ally for 20 (+4% per level). Lasts for 8 seconds.
[E] basic (cooldown 12 s, cost 30): Throw a cloud of wind at the 2 nearest enemies (prioritizing Heroes), dealing 133 (+4% per level) damage. Heroes hit are Blinded and Slowed by 15% for 1.5 seconds.
[R1] heroic (cooldown 20 s, cost 80): Channel for up to 6 seconds. Every 0.25 seconds, increase the cooldown of [R1] by 2 seconds, up to 50 additional seconds, and heal the lowest Health nearby allied Hero for 66 (+4% per level) Health.
[R2] heroic (cooldown 50 s, cost 60): this hero channels for 2 seconds, summoning a [R2] that hits the nearest enemy Hero within 12 range and all enemies near them, dealing 300 (+4% per level) damage and slowing their Movement Speed by 70% for 4 seconds.
[D] trait: Upon taking damage, your Basic Ability cooldowns refresh 50% faster and you gain 10% Movement Speed for 1 second.
