Hero code: K383
Basic attack (level 1 values; several entries mean alternate forms, units or attack modes): range 5.5, one attack every 0.9 s, 60 damage per attack.

ABILITIES
[Q] basic (cooldown 5 s, cost 35): Heal an allied Hero for 380 (+4% per level) Health over 20 seconds.
[W] basic (cooldown 3 s, cost 10): Deal 90 (+4% per level) damage to enemies in an area and reveal them for 2 seconds.  Allies with an active [Q] are healed for 130 (+4% per level) Health for each enemy Hero hit by [W].
[E] basic (cooldown 12 s, cost 65): Root enemy Heroes in an area for 1.25 seconds, and deal 117 (+4% per level) damage over the duration. Affected area grows over 3 seconds.
[R1] heroic (cooldown 80 s, cost 80): Heal nearby allied Heroes for 80 (+4% per level) Health per second for 8 seconds. Allies affected by [Q] within [R1]'s area gain 10 Armor.
[R2] heroic (cooldown 90 s, cost 100): After 0.5 seconds, deal 310 (+4% per level) damage in a large area around this hero, Silencing enemies making them unable to use Abilities for 3 seconds.
[D] trait (cooldown 25 s): Grant an allied Hero 20% of their maximum Mana over 5 seconds. While affected by [D], their Basic Ability cooldowns recharge 50% faster.  Cannot be used on Heroes that do not use Mana.
