Hero code: K983
Basic attack: this hero has no basic attack of its own.

ABILITIES
[Q] basic (cooldown 3 s): Deal 135 (+5% per level) damage to enemies in the area.
[W] basic (cooldown 6 s): Throw a bomb that will bounce three times, dealing 126 (+5% per level) damage to enemies.
[E] basic: Detonate the partner hero's term E, dealing 210 (+4% per level) damage around it.
[R1] heroic (cooldown 80 s): After 1 second, nearby enemies are slowed by 50% while this hero channels, up to 5 seconds. Activate to deal 353 (+5% per level) damage.
[R2] heroic (cooldown 60 s): After 1 second, unleash 20 term C over 4 seconds, each dealing 87 (+4% per level) damage to the first target hit. The bolts fire towards your mouse.
[D] trait (cooldown 5 s): Activate to increase this hero's damage by 25%, but reduce the partner hero's Armor by 25.  Passive: this hero is permanently immune to Stun and Silence effects.
[Z] mount (cooldown 30 s): Nudge the partner hero a small distance and grant him 25% Movement Speed for 2 seconds.
[1] activable (cooldown 45 s): Place an eye, granting vision of a large area around it for 45 seconds. The eye can be killed by enemies with 2 Basic Attacks. Stores up to 2 charges.

TALENTS (one is chosen per level)

  [T1.1] modifies Z: Increases the Movement Speed bonus of Shove by 10% and its duration by 2 seconds.
  [T1.2] modifies Z: Every time the partner hero is hit by a Hero Basic Attack, reduce the cooldown of Shove by 1 second.
  [T1.3] modifies Passive: [1]'s cooldown is decreased by 25 seconds, its range is increased by 33%, and the number of Basic Attacks required to kill it is increased by 1.

  [T4.3] modifies E: Quest: Damaging a Hero with [E] increases its damage by 5, up to 200.  Reward: After damaging 20 Heroes, the range and speed of the partner hero's term E is increased by 20%.
Level 7:

  [T7.3] modifies Trait: Increase the damage bonus of [D] by 5%. Each time this hero damages an enemy Hero this bonus increases by 1% for 4 seconds, up to an additional 10% bonus damage.
Level 10:

Level 13:
