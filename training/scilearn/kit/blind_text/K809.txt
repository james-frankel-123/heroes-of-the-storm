Hero code: K809
Basic attack (level 1 values; several entries mean alternate forms, units or attack modes): range 2, one attack every 1 s, 95 damage per attack.

ABILITIES
[Q] basic (cooldown 9 s, cost 50): Deals 164 (+4% per level) damage to target enemy.  Can be self-cast to heal for 262 (+4% per level) Health.
[W] basic (cooldown 10 s, cost 60): Root enemies within the target area for 1.25 seconds and deals 68 (+4% per level) damage.
[E] basic (cooldown 1 s, cost 13): Deal 40 (+4% per level) damage per second to nearby enemies and Slow their Movement Speed by 10% per second, stacking up to 40%. Heroes hit also have their Attack Speed Slowed by 10% per second, stacking up to 40%. [E]'s effects last for 1.5 seconds.
[R1] heroic (cooldown 80 s, cost 80): Summons term F that last 15 seconds and atttack for 20 (+4% per level) damage. Sacrifice term F to heal for 267 (+4% per level) Health.
[R2] heroic (cooldown 100 s, cost 100): Deals 230 (+4% per level) damage and Slows enemies by 60% for 4 seconds. Also disables Minions, Mercenaries, Monsters and Structures for 20 seconds.
[D] trait (cooldown 10 s): Activate to make this hero's next Basic Attack strike immediately and deal 99 (+4% per level) increased damage. Dealing damage restores 30 Mana.
