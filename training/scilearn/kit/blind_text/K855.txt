Hero code: K855
Basic attack (level 1 values; several entries mean alternate forms, units or attack modes): range 5.5, one attack every 1 s, 120 damage per attack.

ABILITIES
[Q] basic (cooldown 12 s): Launch a grenade that explodes at the end of its path or upon hitting an enemy, dealing 124 (+4% per level) damage to nearby enemies. Grenades can ricochet off of terrain. Deals 50% less damage to Structures.  Stores up to 4 charges. [Q]'s cooldown replenishes all charges at the same time.
[W] basic (cooldown 16 s): Place a mine on the ground. this hero's Trait can be activated to detonate the mine, dealing 180 (+4% per level) damage to nearby enemies and knocking them back. this hero can also be affected by [W], but takes no damage.  Limit 1 active mine.
[E] basic (cooldown 12 s): Place a trap on the ground that arms after 2 seconds. Deals 130 (+4% per level) damage to the first enemy that walks over it and Roots them for 2 seconds.  Limit 1 active trap.
[R1] heroic (cooldown 75 s): Create a motorized bomb with 530 (+4% per level) Health that lasts 15 seconds. While active, this hero is immobile but gains control of [R1]'s movement.  [R1] can be reactivated to detonate immediately, knocking nearby enemies back and dealing 720 (+4% per level) damage to enemies near the center gradually reduced to 445 (+4% per level) to enemies on the edge.
[R2] heroic (cooldown 75 s): After 1.25 seconds, this hero launches into the air. While in the air, he can steer the landing location by moving.  After 3.75 seconds, this hero lands, dealing 935 (+4% per level) damage to nearby enemies and activating [D]. 5 seconds after landing, this hero reappears at the Hall of Storms and gains 150% additional Movement Speed until dismounted.
[D] trait: Upon dying, drop 5 grenades that explode after 0.75 seconds, each dealing 250 (+4% per level) damage to nearby enemies. Deals 75% less damage to Structures.  term B  Detonate an active [W].
