Hero code: K488
Basic attack (level 1 values; several entries mean alternate forms, units or attack modes): range 6.5, one attack every 1 s, 105 damage per attack.

ABILITIES
[Q] basic (cooldown 6 s, cost 50): Deal 230 (+4% per level) damage to the first enemy hit.  Passive: Hitting an enemy Hero with Snipe permanently increases the damage of Snipe by 6%, stacking up to 30%. Gain an additional 25% damage bonus at maximum stacks. All stacks are lost if Snipe fails to hit an enemy.
[W] basic (cooldown 12 s, cost 65): Deal 100 (+4% per level) damage to an enemy and Slow them by 40% for 2.25 seconds.
[E] basic (cooldown 15 s, cost 50): Create a Decoy for 5 seconds with 100% of this hero's current Health that attacks enemies, dealing 10% of this hero's base damage. Whenever a Decoy takes damage, it deals that amount of damage to itself, effectively doubling the damage it takes.  Using this Ability does not break Stealth.
[R1] heroic (cooldown 100 s, cost 100): Locks in on the target Hero, then fires 3 shots that hit the first Hero or Structure they come in contact with for 372 (+4% per level) damage each.
[R2] heroic (cooldown 50 s, cost 100): After a 1.5 second delay, deals 435 (+4% per level) damage to enemies within an area. Unlimited range.
[D] trait: Gain Stealth when out of combat for 3 seconds. Taking damage, attacking, using Abilities, or Channeling ends Stealth. Remaining stationary for at least 1.5 seconds while Stealthed grants Invisible.  Passive: Gain 15% Movement Speed while Stealthed.
[1] activable (cooldown 60 s): Activate to instantly grant Stealth to this hero and spawn a [E] at her location. this hero is Unrevealable for the first 0.5 seconds when Stealthed by [1].
