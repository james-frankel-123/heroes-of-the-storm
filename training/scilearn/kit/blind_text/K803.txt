Hero code: K803
Basic attack (level 1 values; several entries mean alternate forms, units or attack modes): range 5.5, one attack every 0.6 s, 70 damage per attack.

ABILITIES
[Q] basic (cooldown 10 s, cost 60): Fire an arrow that deals 140 (+4% per level) damage to the first target hit, then seeks up to 2 additional enemies, prioritizing Heroes, dealing 80 (+4% per level) damage. Can hit an enemy multiple times.
[W] basic (cooldown 13 s, cost 75): Deal 159 (+4% per level) damage to enemies within the target area.
[E] basic (cooldown 10 s, cost 50): Dash to the target area. this hero's next Basic Attack within 2 seconds deals 6% increased damage per stack of [D].
[R1] heroic (cooldown 60 s, cost 80): Rapidly attack enemies within 10 range for 60 (+4% per level) damage per hit, prioritizing Heroes over Minions. this hero is able to move and use [E] while strafing. Lasts for 4 seconds.
[R2] heroic (cooldown 50 s, cost 50): Launch a wave of term C that deals 250 (+4% per level) damage and Stuns enemies in the target area for 0.5 seconds.  Stores up to 2 charges.
[D] trait: Basic Attacks grant a stack of [D], up to 10. Each [D] stack increases Basic Attack damage by 8% and Movement Speed by 1%. Lasts 6 seconds.
