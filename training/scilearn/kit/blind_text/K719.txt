Hero code: K719
Basic attack (level 1 values; several entries mean alternate forms, units or attack modes): range 1.5, one attack every 1.1 s, 99 damage per attack.

ABILITIES
[Q] basic (cooldown 8 s, cost 40): Step forward dealing 113 (+4% per level) damage and Slowing enemies by 60% decaying over 2 seconds.
[W] basic (cooldown 10 s, cost 50): After 1 second, this hero pulls nearby enemies toward her, Stunning them for 0.25 seconds and dealing 55 (+4% per level) damage. Deals 200% increased damage to Minions and Mercenaries.
[E] basic (cooldown 12 s, cost 45): Deal 59 (+4% per level) damage to enemies and Blind them for 1.5 seconds.
[R1] heroic (cooldown 50 s, cost 60): this hero leaps into the air for 2 seconds and she can steer the landing location by moving. While in the air, allies below this hero are Unstoppable. Upon landing enemies hit take 225 (+4% per level) damage and are Stunned for 0.25 seconds.
[R2] heroic (cooldown 60 s, cost 90): Deal 114 (+4% per level) damage and Stun the first enemy hit for 1.5 seconds. [R2] then bounces to 2 nearby enemies, dealing 57 (+4% per level) damage and Stunning them for 0.75 seconds.
[D] trait (cooldown 22 s): Activate to grant this hero a Shield that absorbs 810 (+4% per level) damage for 4 seconds. While this Shield is active, this hero is Unstoppable.
