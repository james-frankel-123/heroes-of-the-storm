The last spirit Ability that is cast is empowered. If all three spirits are killed, this hero will die as well.  Storm can grant the spirits a Shield.  Earth can leap to an area and Slow enemies.  Fire can grant the spirits Attack Speed, damage.

[W#2] basic (cost 10): Breathe a cone of flames, dealing 85 (+4% per level) damage. Enemies that are soaked in term D are Ignited, dealing 171 (+4% per level) additional damage over 3 seconds and adding 1.5 seconds to the duration of the Slow from [W].   After being used or after 6 seconds, this ability becomes [W].  [W] Damage and Slow enemies.

[alt-W] basic: Fire and Earth leap to the target location, dealing 32 (+4% per level) damage and Slowing enemies in a large area by 70% for 1.5 seconds.  If this is the final Ability cast, increase the damage dealt by 300% and instead of Slowing, the leap Roots enemy Heroes hit for 1.75 seconds.
[alt-E] basic: Increase the Attack Speed and Basic Attack damage of the spirits by 50% for 5 seconds.  If this is the final Ability cast, the Attack Speed and damage bonus is increased to 75% and the spirits gain 50% Movement Speed for its duration.
