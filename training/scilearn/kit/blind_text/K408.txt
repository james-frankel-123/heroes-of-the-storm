Hero code: K408
Basic attack (level 1 values; several entries mean alternate forms, units or attack modes): range 1.25, one attack every 0.8 s, 60 damage per attack.

ABILITIES
[Q] basic (cooldown 4 s): Deal 86 (+4% per level) damage and apply [Q] on nearby enemies for 6 seconds, slowing them by 20%.  Deal 210 (+4% per level) damage to enemies who are already Slimed.
[W] basic (cooldown 15 s): Spit out a [W] with 225 (+5.5% per level) health at the target point. After 3 seconds, the fish will blow up for 410 (+4% per level) damage. Deals 50% less damage to Structures.
[E] basic (cooldown 14 s): Becomes Invulnerable for 2 seconds. While active, this hero cannot attack or use abilities.
[R1] heroic (cooldown 110 s): After 0.75 seconds, this hero commands a legion of term D to march in a target direction, each one leaping onto the first enemy Hero or Structure they find. Each term D deals 125 (+4% per level) damage and slows its target by 15% for 5 seconds. term D deal 50% damage to Structures.
[R2] heroic (cooldown 50 s): this hero becomes Unstoppable and Stuns target enemy Hero for 3 seconds while he hits them for 1 damage a second.
[D] trait (cooldown 15 s): Place an term F at target location, revealing the nearby area. Upon dying, this hero respawns at the term F after 8 seconds. this hero only grants 25% of a real Hero's experience upon dying.  If this hero's term F is killed, he is revealed to enemies for 15 seconds, and [D] is placed on cooldown.
