Hero code: K343
Basic attack (level 1 values; several entries mean alternate forms, units or attack modes): range 6, one attack every 0.75 s, 55 damage per attack.

ABILITIES
[Q] basic (cooldown 16 s, cost 15): Heal an ally Hero for 270 (+4% per level). [Q]'s cooldown is reduced by 1.5 seconds every time this hero hits an enemy with a Basic Attack or damages an enemy Hero.  Stores up to 2 charges. Cooldown replenishes all charges at the same time.
[W] basic (cooldown 15 s, cost 50): Send an term E across the battleground revealing its path, dealing 120 (+4% per level) damage to the first Hero hit, and revealing them for 5 seconds. If a Hero revealed by [W] dies, refresh its cooldown.
[E] basic (cooldown 13 s, cost 60): After 0.75 seconds, deal 150 (+4% per level) damage and Stun enemies in the target area for 0.75 seconds.
[R1] heroic (cooldown 90 s, cost 75): Grant all allied Heroes Stealth for 10 seconds and heal them for 380 (+4% per level) Health over 10 seconds. Remaining stationary for at least 1.5 seconds while Stealthed grants Invisible.
[R2] heroic (cooldown 80 s, cost 75): Deal 92 (+4% per level) damage per second and Slow enemies by 20% in an area. Lasts 6 seconds.
[D] trait (cooldown 20 s): Reveal a non-Structure enemy and reduce their Armor by 15 for 4 seconds.  Passive: this hero's Basic Attacks heal her for 1% of her maximum Health, doubled against targets with [D].
