Hero code: K899
Basic attack (level 1 values; several entries mean alternate forms, units or attack modes): range 1, one attack every 1 s, 75 damage per attack.

ABILITIES
[Q] basic (cooldown 12 s, cost 80): Heal target allied Hero for 362 (+4% per level) Health. When used on a target other than this hero, also heal this hero for 181 (+4% per level) Health.
[W] basic (cooldown 10 s, cost 55): Heal all allied Heroes and Minions in a line for 194 (+4% per level) Health, dealing 177 (+4% per level) damage to enemies.
[E] basic (cooldown 10 s, cost 40): Deal 109 (+4% per level) damage and Stun the target for 1 second.
[R1] heroic (cooldown 90 s, cost 80): Make an allied Hero Invulnerable and increase their Movement Speed by 20% for 3 seconds.
[R2] heroic (cooldown 80 s, cost 80): Deal 170 (+4% per level) damage and Stun nearby enemies for 1.75 seconds.
[D] trait: this hero's Basic Abilities grant 25 Armor to allied Heroes, and 15 to this hero, for 2 seconds.  term A  Upon dying, this hero becomes an Invulnerable spirit for up to 8 seconds. While in spirit form, this hero can heal allies with [alt-Q] for 230 (+4% per level).
Alternate form or second unit abilities:
[alt-Q] basic (cooldown 1.5 s): Heal an ally for 230 (+4% per level) Health.
