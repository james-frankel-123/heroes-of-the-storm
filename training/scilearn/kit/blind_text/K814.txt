ABILITIES

[E] basic (cooldown 12 s, cost 50): Fire a [E] that deals 66 (+4% per level) damage to the first Hero hit and swaps this hero's position with theirs. Hitting a Hero also grants this hero Unstoppable for 1.5 seconds. Can be used during [Q].
[R1] heroic (cooldown 70 s, cost 65): Target an enemy Hero with an orbital beam from the term A, dealing 184 (+4% per level) damage per second for 8 seconds. The beam will chase the target as they move.  Unlimited range.
[R2] heroic (cooldown 50 s, cost 40): Fire a large area pulse from the term A, dealing 114 (+4% per level) damage and Blinding enemies for 4 seconds. Unlimited range.
[D] trait (cooldown 24 s): After taking damage while below 75% Health, this hero gains a 365 (+4% per level) Shield for 5 seconds. Basic Attacks lower the cooldown of [D] by 4 seconds.
