Hero code: K661
Basic attack (level 1 values; several entries mean alternate forms, units or attack modes): range 7, one attack every 1 s, 82 damage per attack.

ABILITIES
[Q] basic (cooldown 3 s, cost 20): After 0.5 seconds, fire a missile that deals 305 (+4% per level) damage to the first enemy hit. Deals 50% damage to Structures.  Casting [Q] leaves an Echo behind that mimics this hero's [Q] and Basic Attack, dealing 40% damage. Maximum of 1 Echo active at a time.
[W] basic (cooldown 14 s, cost 65): Vector Targeting Launch 3 blasts into the air that land every 0.75 seconds towards the targeted direction, dealing 215 (+4% per level) damage each.
[E] basic (cooldown 15 s, cost 20): Place a [E] that arms and Stealths after 2 seconds. this hero's Trait can be activated to detonate the trap, placing all nearby allied or enemy Heroes in Time Stop for 2 seconds. Maximum of 1 trap active at a time.
[R1] heroic (cooldown 50 s, cost 60): Choose an enemy Hero. After 3 seconds, they are teleported back to the location where [R1] was cast on them.  Basic Abilities recharge 500% faster for 3 seconds after casting [R1].
[R2] heroic (cooldown 5 s, cost 5): Summon a sand vortex that Slows enemies by 10% every 0.25 seconds, up to 70%.
[D] trait: this hero has traveled to the future, and as such, will learn her Talents 2 levels earlier than her teammates!  Detonate [E]  Detonates active [E].
