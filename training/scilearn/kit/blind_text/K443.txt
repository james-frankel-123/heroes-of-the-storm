Hero code: K443
Basic attack (level 1 values; several entries mean alternate forms, units or attack modes): range 5.5, one attack every 0.6 s, 81 damage per attack.

ABILITIES
[Q] basic (cooldown 8 s, cost 25): Shoot the closest enemy up to 5 times over 1.5 seconds for 39 (+4% per level) damage, prioritizing Heroes. Cooldown is reset on getting a Takedown.
[W] basic (cooldown 11 s, cost 50): Throw a dagger at a target enemy that deals 30 (+4% per level) damage and an additional 150 (+4% per level) damage over 2.5 seconds. Damage dealt by this hero to the initial target spreads [W] to all nearby enemies.
[E] basic (cooldown 11 s, cost 75): Send forth a wave of banshees dealing 114 (+4% per level) damage to all targets. Reactivate to teleport to the banshees' location.
[R1] heroic (cooldown 90 s, cost 100): Shoot an arrow that can be reactivated to deal 228 (+4% per level) damage and Silence enemies in an area for 2.5 seconds. The arrow detonates automatically if it reaches maximum range.
[R2] heroic (cooldown 40 s, cost 50): After 0.25 seconds, fire a missile that [R2] the first enemy Hero hit. Heroes hit are Silenced, Slowed by 30%, have 3 stacks of term B applied to them, and are forced to walk towards this hero for 1.75 seconds.
[D] trait (cooldown 40 s): Activate to cause all Basic Attacks and Abilities to Stun Minions, non-Elite Mercenaries, and Structures for 3 seconds.
