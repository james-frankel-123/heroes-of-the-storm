Hero code: K285
Basic attack (level 1 values; several entries mean alternate forms, units or attack modes): range 1.25, one attack every 0.8 s, 74 damage per attack.

ABILITIES
[Q] basic (cooldown 8 s, cost 50): Create a shockwave that travels in a straight line, dealing 150 (+4% per level) damage and Slowing enemies by 35% for 1.5 seconds. Each enemy hit heals this hero for 35 (+4% per level), increased to 140 (+4% per level) against Heroes.
[W] basic (cooldown 10 s, cost 30): Parry all incoming Basic Attacks for 1.25 seconds, reducing their damage by 100%.  Stores up to 2 charges.
[E] basic (cooldown 12 s, cost 45): Charge to the target enemy, dealing 50 (+4% per level) damage and Slowing them by 75% for 1 second.
[R1] heroic (cooldown 16 s, cost 20): Silence a target Hero and force them to attack this hero for 1.25 seconds.  Passive: Maximum Health and Health Regeneration increased by 40%.
[R2] heroic (cooldown 20 s, cost 40): Smash a target enemy, dealing 185 (+4% per level) damage and lowering their Armor by 25 for 3 seconds, causing them to take 25% increased damage.  Passive: Base Attack Damage increased by 100%. Passive: Maximum Health and Health Regeneration reduced by 10%.
[R3] heroic: Basic Attacks reduce [D]'s cooldown by 9 seconds, and increase this hero's Movement Speed by 30% for 2 seconds.  Passive: Attack Speed increased by 100%. Passive: Base Attack Damage reduced by 25%.
[D] trait (cooldown 18 s): Every 18 seconds, this hero's next Basic Attack deals 125 (+4% per level) bonus Spell Damage. Basic Attacks reduce this cooldown by 3 seconds.
