Hero code: K784
Basic attack (level 1 values; several entries mean alternate forms, units or attack modes): range 1.5, one attack every 1.1 s, 173 damage per attack.

ABILITIES
[Q] basic (cooldown 7 s, cost 40): Shock an enemy with lightning, dealing 162 (+4% per level) damage. The lightning then bounces 3 times to nearby enemies, dealing 81 (+4% per level) damage to each enemy hit.
[W] basic (cooldown 12 s, cost 40): Unleash a [W] that deals 153 (+4% per level) damage to enemies in its path and Roots Heroes hit for 1 second. Each Hero hit increases the distance traveled by 25%.
[E] basic (cooldown 12 s, cost 60): Increase this hero's Movement Speed by 30% for 4 seconds. His next 3 Basic Attacks occur 100% faster and generate stacks of [D].
[R1] heroic (cooldown 70 s, cost 75): After 0.5 seconds, sunder the earth in a long line, dealing 290 (+4% per level) damage and shoving enemies to the side, Stunning them for 1 second.
[R2] heroic (cooldown 100 s, cost 90): After 0.5 seconds, summon a massive [R2] that pulses every 4 seconds. Each pulse lasts 2 seconds, Slowing all enemies in the area by 50%, and deals 50 (+4% per level) damage to enemy Heroes. Does 3 pulses.
[D] trait: Dealing damage with Abilities grants 1 stack of [D]. At 5 stacks, this hero is instantly healed for 223 (+4% per level) Health.
