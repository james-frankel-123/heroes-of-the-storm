ABILITIES
[Q] basic (cooldown 7 s): Sacrifice 15% of this hero's current Health, healing an allied Hero for 150% of that amount.  Passive: Increase this hero's Health regeneration by 150%.  [D]: [alt-Q] Cooldown greatly reduced and does not cost Health.
[W] basic (cooldown 14 s, cost 75): Plant a seed of healing that blooms after 3 seconds, healing nearby allied Heroes for 20% of their maximum Health.  [D]: [alt-W] Heal area and amount greatly increased.
[E] basic (cooldown 2 s, cost 50): Launch a fireball, Burning enemies hit for 75 (+4% per level) damage over 5.5 seconds.  Hitting enemies that are already Burning deals 125 (+4% per level) bonus damage upon impact, Slows them by 40% decaying over 2 seconds, and refunds the Mana cost.  [D]: [alt-E] Damage and Knockback enemies in an arc.
[R1] heroic (cooldown 50 s, cost 50): Bind this hero's life force with an allied Hero. Both her and her target are healed for 480 (+4% per level) Health over 2 seconds. Afterwards, the Hero with a lower percentage of Health is healed to the same Health percentage as the other Hero.
[R2] heroic (cooldown 100 s, cost 100): After 1.25 seconds, take to the sky and drop 5 fireballs over 6 seconds at the position of the mouse cursor. term D deal 135 (+4% per level) damage to enemies and heal allied Heroes for 300 (+4% per level) Health.  2 seconds after dropping all fireballs, this hero lands at the position of the mouse cursor.
[D] trait (cooldown 150 s): After 1.25 seconds, transform into a dragon and gain 500 (+4% per level) Health.  While [D] is active, this hero's Abilities are empowered, and her Basic Attacks deal 143 (+4% per level) damage and heal allied Heroes for 43 (+4% per level) in an arc in front of her. Additionally, the duration of incoming Stuns, Roots, and Slows, is reduced by 50%.  Lasts 15 seconds.
Alternate form or second unit abilities:
[alt-Q] basic (cooldown 3 s): Heal an allied Hero for 20% of this hero's current Health.
[alt-W] basic (cooldown 16 s): Plant a seed of healing that blooms after 3 seconds, healing nearby allied Heroes for 30% of their maximum Health.
[alt-E] basic (cooldown 4 s): Deal 150 (+4% per level) damage to enemies in an area, knocking them back and Slowing them by 50% for 3 seconds.
[alt-D] trait: this hero has transformed into a powerful dragon aspect, gaining 500 (+4% per level) Health, resistance to disabling effects, and empowered Basic Attacks and Abilities.
[alt-R] heroic (cooldown 100 s, cost 100): Take to the sky and drop 5 fireballs over 6 seconds at the position of the mouse cursor. term D deal 135 (+4% per level) damage to enemies and heal allied Heroes for 300 (+4% per level) Health.  2 seconds after dropping all fireballs, this hero lands at the position of the mouse cursor.
