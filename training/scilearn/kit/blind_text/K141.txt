Hero code: K141
Basic attack (level 1 values; several entries mean alternate forms, units or attack modes): range 4.5, one attack every 1 s, 66 damage per attack.

ABILITIES
[Q] basic (cooldown 8 s, cost 20): Conjure a skull that travels in the target direction after 0.625 seconds, dealing 127 (+4% per level) damage to enemies hit and Slowing Heroes hit by 25% for 2 seconds.
[W] basic (cooldown 14 s, cost 50): A ring of lightning appears around this hero for 2.5 seconds. Enemies within the ring take 43 (+4% per level) damage every 0.25 seconds.  Each time a cast of [W] hits a Hero, its damage is increased by 3%, up to 30%.
[E] basic (cooldown 18 s, cost 35): Teleport to a location, dealing 78 (+4% per level) damage to nearby enemies and leaving behind a [E] at this hero's original location.  After 2.5 seconds, this hero is teleported back to the Shade's location.
[R1] heroic (cooldown 120 s, cost 80): Channel for 2.5 seconds, revealing all enemy Heroes. After the Channel completes, all enemy Heroes take 357 (+4% per level) damage and are Slowed by 25% for 2.5 seconds.
[R2] heroic (cooldown 50 s, cost 50): After 1 second, unleash a wave of evil spirits that Root the first enemy Hero hit for 2.5 seconds and deal 250 (+4% per level) damage to them over the same duration.  [R2] spreads outwards from its initial target, Rooting and damaging additional nearby enemy Heroes.
[D] trait: Hitting enemy Heroes with Basic Abilities reduces this hero's Basic Ability cooldowns.  [Q] and [E] grant 1.5 seconds of cooldown reduction per Hero hit, and [W] grants 0.3 seconds per Hero hit.
