Hero code: K295
Basic attack (level 1 values; several entries mean alternate forms, units or attack modes): range 5.5, one attack every 1 s, 85 damage per attack.

ABILITIES
[Q] basic (cooldown 5 s, cost 20): Cast for 0.75 seconds to heal an allied Hero for 280 (+4% per level).
[W] basic (cooldown 10 s, cost 50): Send light that deals 140 (+4% per level) damage to enemies and then returns to this hero, healing allied Heroes for 130 (+4% per level) in a wider path. Healing increases by 25% per enemy Hero hit.
[E] basic (cooldown 9 s, cost 40): Shove a swell of light forward, dealing 175 (+4% per level) damage to the first enemy Hero hit and Rooting them for 1.25 seconds.
[R1] heroic (cooldown 80 s, cost 70): After 0.5 seconds, Channel to invoke the Light for 3 seconds. While nearby, allied Heroes heal for up to 30% of their max Health and are Protected.
[R2] heroic (cooldown 60 s, cost 70): Imbue an allied Hero with the Light. After 1.5 seconds, it explodes, dealing 150 (+4% per level) damage to enemies and Stunning them for 1.25 seconds.  The target gains a Shield that absorbs 165 (+4% per level) damage per enemy Hero hit. Lasts for 5 seconds.
[D] trait (cooldown 70 s): Faith instantly pulls an allied Hero to this hero's location, granting them Unstoppable while they travel.  term A  Basic Attacks against enemy Heroes heal the lowest Health allied Hero near this hero for 32 (+4% per level).
