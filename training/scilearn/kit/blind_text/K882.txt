Hero code: K882
Basic attack (level 1 values; several entries mean alternate forms, units or attack modes): range 4.5, one attack every 0.25 s, 20 damage per attack.

ABILITIES
[Q] basic (cooldown 8 s): Launch a [Q] that deals 75 (+4% per level) damage to enemies within the area. Deals 50% damage to Structures.  Stores up to 4 charges.
[W] basic (cooldown 10 s): Gain a Shield that absorbs 560 (+4% per level) damage for 3 seconds.
[E] basic (cooldown 12 s): Grants an allied Hero a Shield that absorbs 420 (+4% per level) damage for 3 seconds.
[R1] heroic (cooldown 60 s): Launch a gravity bomb that detonates after 1 second and draws enemy Heroes toward the center for 2.5 seconds. Affected Heroes take 30 (+4% per level) damage a second.
[R2] heroic (cooldown 45 s): Launch a gravity bomb that deals 124 (+4% per level) damage and creates an [R2] for 3.5 seconds. Enemies who enter the affected area are knocked back and have their Movement Speed reduced by 50% for 1 second.
[D] trait: Each time this hero's [W] or [E] absorbs 9 (+4% per level) damage, her Energy is increased by 1. Each point of Energy increases this hero's damage by 2%. After 0.5 seconds, Energy decays by 3 per second.
