Hero code: K925
Basic attack (level 1 values; several entries mean alternate forms, units or attack modes): range 1.5, one attack every 0.9 s, 145 damage per attack.

ABILITIES
[Q] basic (cooldown 4 s, cost 30): Deal 152 (+4% per level) damage to enemies in a crescent area.  Hitting at least 2 enemy Heroes with [Q] reduces its cooldown to 0.5 seconds, and refunds its Mana cost.
[W] basic (cooldown 14 s, cost 55): this hero's next Basic Attack cleaves and applies a tether to enemy Heroes hit for 2.5 seconds. If a tethered Hero moves too far from this hero, they are pulled toward her, dealing 110 (+4% per level) damage and breaking the tether.
[E] basic (cooldown 13 s, cost 50): Send a shadow of this hero outward that will return to its cast location, dealing 150 (+4% per level) damage to enemies along both paths. If an enemy Hero is hit, reduce the cooldown by 4 seconds.
[R1] heroic (cooldown 40 s, cost 50): Throw a glaive in the target direction. If an enemy Hero is hit, [R1] can be reactivated to remove their vision and Time Stop them for 4 seconds. Leaving Containment Silences the enemy Hero for 1.5 seconds.  [R1] automatically activates 6 seconds after hitting a Hero.
[R2] heroic (cooldown 100 s, cost 80): Summon 8 term C as a cage around this hero. After 1.5 seconds, enemy Heroes that come in contact with an Avatar consume it and are knocked to the center of the cage. term C last 5 seconds.
[D] trait (cooldown 13 s): Leap into the air, becoming Immune to all hostile effects for 0.75 seconds.  After reaching Level 20, [D] gains an additional charge.
