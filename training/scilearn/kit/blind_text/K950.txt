[W] basic (cooldown 7 s, cost 25): Gain a Shield that grants 40 Spell Armor and absorbs 340 (+4% per level) damage over 3 seconds.

[R1] heroic (cooldown 100 s, cost 75): Deal 68 (+4% per level) damage per second to nearby enemies. Each enemy damaged restores 24 (+4% per level) Health. Lasts 6 seconds.
[R2] heroic (cooldown 70 s, cost 70): Wraps target enemy Hero in a cocoon, rendering them unable to act or be targeted for 7 seconds. Allies of the Hero can attack the cocoon to break it and free them early.
[D] trait (cooldown 8 s): Using an Ability spawns a term C which lasts for 8 seconds, attacking nearby enemies for 20 (+4% per level) damage.
