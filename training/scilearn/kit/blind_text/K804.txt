ABILITIES
[Q] basic (cooldown 4 s, cost 30): Fire two streams that deal 76 (+4% per level) damage to enemies hit, and an additional 120 (+4% per level) damage to enemies hit by both streams. [Q] Ignites [W] it comes in contact with.
[W] basic (cooldown 12 s, cost 50): Vector Targeting Dispense a slick of oil that lasts for 5 seconds and Slows enemies that come in contact with it by 50%.  [W] are Ignited for 2.5 seconds when hit by [Q]. Ignited [W] no longer Slow enemies, but instead deal 16 (+4% per level) damage to them every 0.3 seconds. Additionally, this hero is healed for 49 (+4% per level) Health every 0.3 seconds while standing in Ignited [W].  Stores up to 2 charges.
[E] basic (cooldown 10 s, cost 45): After 0.375 seconds, charge forward. Colliding with an enemy Hero deals 52 (+4% per level) damage to all nearby enemy Heroes and Stuns them for 1.25 seconds.
[R1] heroic (cooldown 70 s, cost 70): After 0.5 seconds, deploy and enter a term C with 1435 (+4% per level) Health. this hero and his allies can enter and exit the term C at will. While in the term C, occupants gain access to term A, dealing 170 (+4% per level) damage to enemies in a line.  Exiting the term C grants 25 Armor for 2 seconds. term C last 10 seconds, or until destroyed.
[R2] heroic (cooldown 80 s, cost 70): Channel for up to 2.6 seconds. Upon ending, Slow nearby enemies by 60% and deal 55 (+4% per level) damage to them every 0.5 seconds. [R2]'s Slow and damage over time duration is extended the longer this hero Channels, from 1 second up to 5 seconds.  this hero's Movement Speed is reduced by 40% while Channeling.
[D] trait (cooldown 90 s): Gain 40 Armor and deal 40 (+4% per level) damage to nearby enemies every 0.5 seconds for 4 seconds.  Each Hero hit by [Q] reduces [D]'s cooldown by 4 seconds.
