Hero code: K288
Basic attack (level 1 values; several entries mean alternate forms, units or attack modes): range 3.75, one attack every 0.25 s, 22 damage per attack; range 5.5, one attack every 0.25 s, 55 damage per attack.

ABILITIES
[Q] basic (cooldown 10 s): Increase this hero's Movement Speed by 115% for 1.75 seconds. Enemies that are hit take 135 (+4% per level) damage and are knocked away.  this hero cannot be Slowed while [Q] are active, and each enemy can only be hit once per use.
[W] basic (cooldown 12 s): Project a defensive field in the target direction for 3 seconds, reducing the damage dealt by enemy Heroes inside it by 50%.  Activate again to change the angle of projection.  Damage dealt to the Mech from enemies within [W] still grants the same amount of [E] Charge.
[E] basic: Eject from the Mech, setting it to self-destruct after 4 seconds. Deals 400 (+4% per level) to 1100 (+4% per level) damage in a large area, depending on distance from center. Deals 50% damage against Structures.  Gain 1% Charge for every 2 seconds spent Basic Attacking, and 25% Charge per 100% of Mech Health lost.
[R1] heroic (cooldown 70 s): this hero's Mech becomes Unstoppable and stomps every 0.5 seconds, dealing 70 (+4% per level) damage in a large area. Every 4th consecutive hit on a Hero deals 100% more damage and Stuns them for 0.5 seconds.  Lasts for 4 seconds.  Requires [D].
[R2] heroic (cooldown 20 s): After .375 seconds, Channel to launch a volley of 12 missiles in a target direction. Each missile impacts the first enemy in its path, dealing 40 (+4% per level) damage in a small area and Slowing by 30% for 2 seconds.  Stores 2 charges with a 5 second cooldown between each use.  Requires [D].
[D] trait: When this hero's Mech dies, she is ejected out after 0.75 seconds and can continue to fight. this hero's Mech only awards 50% of a normal Hero's experience upon dying.  term A  this hero's Mech Basic Attacks deal 22 (+4% per level) damage in a large area every 0.25 seconds. Deals 40% bonus damage against enemies very close to the Mech.
[Z] mount: this hero's Mech can shoot while moving. While shooting or channeling an ability, the Mech's Movement Speed is quickly reduced by 25%.
