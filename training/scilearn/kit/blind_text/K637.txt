Hero code: K637
Basic attack (level 1 values; several entries mean alternate forms, units or attack modes): range 5.5, one attack every 1 s, 63 damage per attack.

ABILITIES
[Q] basic (cooldown 3 s, cost 20): Fire three missiles toward an area, each dealing 147 (+3.5% per level) damage to the first enemy hit. These missiles do 50% damage to Structures.
[W] basic (cooldown 8 s, cost 40): Fire an Orb that powers up as it travels, dealing 135 (+3% per level) damage to the first enemy hit. Damage is increased the further it travels, up to 270 (+3% per level) more damage.
[E] basic (cooldown 5 s, cost 30): Teleport a short distance instantly.
[R1] heroic (cooldown 20 s, cost 80): Channel a powerful beam, dealing 480 (+5% per level) damage over 2.6 seconds to enemies while they are in it. The direction of the beam changes with your mouse cursor position.
[R2] heroic (cooldown 30 s, cost 80): Knock away all enemies from an area and deal 160 (+5% per level) damage.
[D] trait: Getting a Takedown will refresh the cooldown on all of this hero's Abilities.
