Hero code: K666
Basic attack (level 1 values; several entries mean alternate forms, units or attack modes): range 5.5, one attack every 0.8 s, 64 damage per attack.

ABILITIES
[Q] basic (cooldown 8 s): Sweep the area with sacred power, dealing 40 (+4% per level) damage to enemies and an additional 160 (+4% per level) damage to enemies caught in the center.
[W] basic (cooldown 4 s): Consume this hero's stored energy and heal allied Heroes in the area for the amount of energy consumed.
[E] basic (cooldown 12 s): Deal 55 (+4% per level) damage to the first enemy Hero hit and knock them back. If they collide with terrain, they are also stunned for 1.25 seconds and take an additional 165 (+4% per level) damage.
[R1] heroic (cooldown 100 s): Channel on the spirit of a dead ally for 1 second. After a 5 second delay, they are brought back to life with 100% of their maximum Health at the location where they died.  this hero can use this ability while dead to resurrect herself.
[R2] heroic (cooldown 60 s): Place an allied Hero into Stasis for 2 seconds. Upon expiration, [R2] deals 255 (+4% per level) damage to all nearby enemies.
[D] trait (cooldown 1.5 s): Passive: 60% of the damage this hero deals to Heroes and 20% dealt to non-Heroes is stored as energy.  Bestow an allied Hero with term B.  While they remain near this hero, 30% of their damage to Heroes and 10% of their damage to non-Heroes is converted to energy. this hero can only have [D] on 1 ally at a time.  this hero can store up to 475 (+4% per level) energy.
