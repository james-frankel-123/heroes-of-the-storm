Hero code: K431
Basic attack (level 1 values; several entries mean alternate forms, units or attack modes): range 1.5, one attack every 0.8 s, 99 damage per attack.

ABILITIES
[Q] basic (cooldown 12 s, cost 60): Slide to a location dealing 105 (+4% per level) damage and Stunning enemies hit for 1.25 seconds.
[W] basic (cooldown 12 s, cost 55): Deals 68 (+4% per level) damage to nearby enemies, knocking them back.
[E] basic (cooldown 9 s, cost 40): Regenerate 66 (+4% per level) Health per second for 4 seconds.
[R1] heroic (cooldown 120 s, cost 100): After 0.75 seconds, channel to stun nearby enemies for 4 seconds.
[R2] heroic (cooldown 75 s, cost 70): Leap to target location, landing after 2.75 seconds, dealing 330 (+4% per level) damage to enemies in the area, and slowing them by 50% for 4 seconds.
[D] trait: Using a Basic Ability grants 20 Armor for 2 seconds. Heroic Abilities grant 60 Armor.
