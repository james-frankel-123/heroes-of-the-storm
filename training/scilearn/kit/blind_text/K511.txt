Hero code: K511
Basic attack (level 1 values; several entries mean alternate forms, units or attack modes): range 1.5, one attack every 1.1 s, 82 damage per attack.

ABILITIES
[Q] basic (cooldown 2 s, cost 25): Extract the souls of nearby enemies afflicted by [D], dealing 100 (+4% per level) damage and healing this hero for 25 (+4% per level) per target hit. Heroic targets heal this hero for an additional 4% of the Hero's maximum Health.
[W] basic (cooldown 5 s, cost 30): Instantly teleport through an enemy afflicted by [D], dealing 59 (+4% per level) damage and refreshing [D].
[E] basic (cooldown 8 s, cost 50): After 0.25 seconds, unleash a wave of dark mist that applies [D] to enemies it hits.
[R1] heroic (cooldown 60 s, cost 20): Unleash a torrent of souls, continually applying [D] to nearby enemies for 4 seconds.  When [R1] is cast and when it expires, reset the cooldown of [W].
[R2] heroic (cooldown 70 s, cost 100): Apply a death sentence to an enemy Hero that, after 2 seconds, deals damage equal to 50% of their missing Health.  Quest: Enemies killed between the application of [R2] and within 1.5 seconds of it dealing damage permanently reduce its cooldown by 5 seconds, to a minimum of 20 seconds.
[D] trait: Basic Attacks cleave in an area in front of this hero and afflict non-Structure targets with [D] for 4 seconds.  Marked enemies are revealed and take damage equal to 2% of their maximum Health every 1 second.
