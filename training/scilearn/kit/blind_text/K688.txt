Hero code: K688
Basic attack (level 1 values; several entries mean alternate forms, units or attack modes): range 4.5, one attack every 1 s, 75 damage per attack.

ABILITIES
[Q] basic (cooldown 10 s, cost 35): Throw a snowball that hits all enemies in an area. Enemies hit take 70 (+4% per level) damage, are Slowed by 35%, and are Blinded for 1.75 seconds.
[W] basic (cooldown 14 s, cost 70): Command Snowball to create a [W] at the target location for 2 seconds. Enemies within the blizzard take 13 (+4% per level) damage every 0.25 seconds and are Slowed by 7%, stacking up to 35%. When the [W] ends, enemies within its area take an additional 75 (+4% per level) damage and are Stunned for 1.25 seconds.
[E] basic (cooldown 14 s, cost 70): Slide quickly in the targeted direction. When this effect ends, enemies near this hero are knocked back, take 52 (+4% per level) damage, and are Slowed by 80%, rapidly decaying over 1.5 seconds.  Can be canceled during sliding.
[R1] heroic (cooldown 90 s, cost 80): After 0.5 seconds, roll a massive snowball that consumes enemy Heroes in its path. At the end of its path, the snowball crumbles, releasing the consumed Heroes, dealing 220 (+4% per level) damage and Stunning them for 0.5 seconds per Hero consumed.
[R2] heroic (cooldown 60 s, cost 50): Vector Targeting After a 0.75 second delay, create an impassable wall that traps enemy Heroes hit for 2.5 seconds. When the wall expires, trapped Heroes are Slowed by 75%, decaying over 3 seconds.
[D] trait (cooldown 40 s): Become encased in ice for 3 seconds, gaining a rapidly decaying Shield that absorbs up to 1104 (+4% per level) damage. While active, this Shield grants Unstoppable and restores up to 35% of this hero's maximum Health.
