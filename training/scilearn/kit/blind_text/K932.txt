Hero code: K932
Basic attack (level 1 values; several entries mean alternate forms, units or attack modes): range 1.5, one attack every 1.1 s, 71 damage per attack.

ABILITIES
[Q] basic (cooldown 16 s, cost 75): Pull the first enemy hit towards this hero and deal 91 (+4% per level) damage.  After reaching level 13, Hook's range is increased by 40%.
[W] basic (cooldown 8 s, cost 35): Deal 104 (+4% per level) damage to enemies within the target area. Enemies in the inner impact take 50% more damage and are Slowed by 45% for 2 seconds.
[E] basic (cooldown 20 s, cost 55): Deal 319 (+4% per level) damage to non-Heroic units, or 114 (+4% per level) damage to Heroes. Restores 20% of this hero's maximum Health.
[R1] heroic (cooldown 75 s, cost 75): Emit bile that deals 37 (+4% per level) damage per second to enemies within, Slowing them by 35% for 1.5 seconds. Gain 20% Movement Speed while emitting bile. Lasts 8 seconds.
[R2] heroic (cooldown 65 s, cost 70): Consume an enemy Hero, trapping them for 4 seconds. When [R2] ends, the enemy Hero takes 274 (+4% per level) damage. The trapped Hero cannot move or act and doesn't take damage from other sources.
[D] trait (cooldown 60 s): Activate to spread term A in a large area every second and prevent your Movement Speed from being reduced below 110% for 4 seconds.  term A Hitting enemies with Basic Attacks or the inner area of Slam afflicts them with term A, dealing an additional 36 (+4% per level) damage over 4 seconds. Deals 150% more damage to Minions, Mercenaries, and Monsters.
[1] activable (cooldown 16 s, cost 75): Activate to use Hook that can hit allied Heroes. Hitting an allied Hero this way reduces the cooldown of Hook by 8 seconds.  After reaching level 13, [1]'s range is increased by 40%.
