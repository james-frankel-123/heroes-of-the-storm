Hero code: K920
Basic attack (level 1 values; several entries mean alternate forms, units or attack modes): range 1.6, one attack every 0.9 s, 90 damage per attack; range 5.5, one attack every 0.9 s, 90 damage per attack.

ABILITIES
[Q] basic (cooldown 8 s, cost 60): After 0.5 seconds, cause an area to explode with pollen dealing 160 (+4% per level) damage.
[W] basic (cooldown 10 s, cost 50): Enemies currently afflicted by [D] have its duration increased by 3 seconds and are Slowed by 40% decaying over 3 seconds.
[E] basic (cooldown 30 s): Spawn a [E] to scout an area. Can be redirected once active. When the [E] is in a bush for more 2 seconds, its vision radius is increased by 75%.  Lasts 45 seconds.
[R1] heroic (cooldown 15 s, cost 35): Send forth vines that deal 184 (+4% per level) damage to all enemies in a line.  Stores up to 3 charges.
[R2] heroic (cooldown 20 s, cost 50): Leap over an enemy, Slowing them by 80% for 0.35 seconds and dealing 271 (+4% per level) damage.  Stores up to 2 charges.
[D] trait: this hero's Basic Attacks and damaging Abilities poison their target, dealing 33 (+4% per level) damage a second for 3 seconds. Every additional application increases the duration by 3 seconds, up to a maximum of 9 seconds.
[Z] mount: this hero moves 20% faster by leaping short distances.
