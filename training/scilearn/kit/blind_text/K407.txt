Hero code: K407
Basic attack (level 1 values; several entries mean alternate forms, units or attack modes): range 1.5, one attack every 0.9 s, 126 damage per attack; range 0.75, one attack every 0.9 s, 126 damage per attack.

ABILITIES
[Q] basic (cooldown 6 s, cost 40): Deal 200 (+4% per level) damage to nearby enemies.
[W] basic (cooldown 12 s, cost 70): Flings a [W] that sticks to the first enemy hit. Deals 240 (+4% per level) damage after 1 second and Slows the enemy by 40% for 3 seconds.
[E] basic (cooldown 10 s, cost 50): Teleport to the target location.  Using this Ability does not break Stealth.
[R1] heroic (cooldown 20 s, cost 30): Activate to cast an untalented version of this hero's most recently used Basic Ability, dealing 50% less damage.  Passive: After using an Ability, this hero's next Basic Attack within 6 seconds deals 30% more damage.
[R2] heroic (cooldown 100 s, cost 80): Slows time in an area to a near standstill, placing allies and enemies in Time Stop for 5 seconds. this hero is not affected.
[D] trait: Gain Stealth when out of combat for 3 seconds. Taking damage, attacking, using Abilities, or Channeling ends Stealth. Remaining stationary for at least 1.5 seconds while Stealthed grants Invisible.
[1] activable (cooldown 15 s): Activate to teleport to this hero's last non-structure Basic Attack target within 3 seconds. The target is revealed during these 3 seconds.
