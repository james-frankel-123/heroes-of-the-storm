Hero code: K382
Basic attack (level 1 values; several entries mean alternate forms, units or attack modes): range 1.5, one attack every 0.9 s, 110 damage per attack.

ABILITIES
[Q] basic (cooldown 8 s, cost 60): Heal an ally with a wave of healing for 260 (+4% per level) Health. The wave then bounces 2 times to nearby allies within 7 range, restoring 260 (+4% per level) Health to them.
[W] basic (cooldown 8 s, cost 50): Imbue an ally with lightning dealing 64 (+4% per level) damage a second to nearby enemies. Each enemy hit restores 2 Mana to this hero. Lasts 5 seconds.
[E] basic (cooldown 15 s, cost 50): Create a totem that Slows nearby enemies by 35%. The totem has 260 (+4% per level) Health and lasts for 6 seconds.
[R1] heroic (cooldown 90 s, cost 70): Grant nearby allied Heroes 40% Attack Speed and 35% Movement Speed and causes them to heal for 30% of the Basic Attack damage to their primary target. Lasts for 6 seconds.
[R2] heroic (cooldown 100 s, cost 80): After 1 second, heal an allied Hero for 1180 (+4% per level) Health.
[D] trait (cooldown 60 s): When used on an allied Hero, make them Unstoppable for 0.5 seconds. When used on an enemy Hero, Slow them by 80%, decaying over 2 seconds.  Cannot be cast on yourself.
[Z] mount (cooldown 4 s): Instead of using a mount, this hero transforms into a [Z] with 20% increased Movement Speed. Basic Attacks in [Z] form cause him to lunge at his target and deal 75% bonus damage. Dealing damage, using Abilities, and channeling cancels [Z] form.
