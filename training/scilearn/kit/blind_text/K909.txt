Hero code: K909
Basic attack (level 1 values; several entries mean alternate forms, units or attack modes): range 5.5, one attack every 1 s, 83 damage per attack.

ABILITIES
[Q] basic (cooldown 0.5 s, cost 6): Heal target allied Hero or Minion for 172 (+4% per level) Health per second as long as they are in range. After not channeling [Q] for 2 seconds, regenerate 6 Energy per second.  Reactivate to switch targets, or self-cast to cancel channeling.
[W] basic (cooldown 11 s): Grant target ally Hero 35 Armor for 3 seconds.
[E] basic (cooldown 12 s): Fire a grenade that can be manually detonated, dealing 208 (+4% per level) to nearby enemies and knocking them away.
[R1] heroic (cooldown 70 s): Grant an allied Hero 75% Attack Speed and 25% Movement Speed for 10 seconds.
[R2] heroic (cooldown 45 s): Target a location for a term A transport. For up to 10 seconds before takeoff, allies can right-click to enter the term A.
[D] trait: While channeling [Q], this hero regenerates 2% of her maximum Health per second.
