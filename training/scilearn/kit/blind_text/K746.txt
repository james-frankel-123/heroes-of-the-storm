Hero code: K746
Basic attack (level 1 values; several entries mean alternate forms, units or attack modes): range 2.5, one attack every 1.5 s, 155 damage per attack.

ABILITIES
[Q] basic (cooldown 3 s, cost 10): After 1.5 seconds, this hero breathes a stream of flame, dealing 21 (+4% per level) damage every 0.125 seconds. Drains 30 Energy per second.
[W] basic (cooldown 4 s, cost 20): After 0.75 seconds, this hero flaps his wings, dealing 65 (+4% per level) damage to nearby enemies.  [alt-W]: [alt-W#2] Create a lava pool that damages and Slows
[E] basic (cooldown 5 s, cost 20): After 0.5 seconds, this hero lunges in a direction dealing 38 (+4% per level) damage to enemies and Slowing them by 35% for 2 seconds. this hero bites enemies at the end of the lunge, dealing an additional 72 (+4% per level) damage.  [alt-W]: [alt-E] Damage and Stun enemies in two lines
[R1] heroic (cooldown 90 s): After 2 seconds, this hero flies in the targeted direction, dealing 90 (+4% per level) damage on impact and leaving scorched ground that deals 36 (+4% per level) damage a second. Lasts 6 seconds.
[D] trait: this hero is fitted with 4 term C which grant 10 Armor each. Every 25% Health lost causes this hero to lose a plate. Plates are only restored when [Z] is used, based on the amount of Health this hero has when landing.  this hero is also a mighty term B who is permanently Unstoppable and deals 100% additional Spell Damage to Heroes, but views his allies with contempt and ignores any of their friendly effects.
[Z] mount (cooldown 45 s): After 3 seconds, fly into the sky, becoming Immune to all effects and gaining 2.5% Health per second. Once cast, this ability cannot be interrupted. Cannot be cast for 4 seconds after taking damage, Basic Attacking or casting an Ability.
[1] activable (cooldown 20 s): Switches this hero between [alt-Q] and [alt-W] forms, each granting access to two unique Basic Abilities.  [W] damages enemies around this hero and [E] allows him to lunge at enemies.  [alt-W#2] creates a Slowing area and [alt-E] Stuns enemies in two lines.
Alternate form or second unit abilities:
[alt-Q] basic (cooldown 8 s): After 1.75 seconds, this hero slams down to the ground, dealing 50 (+4% per level) damage to enemies in the area and landing in [alt-Q] form. this hero can begin to cast Abilities 3 seconds after landing.
[alt-W] basic (cooldown 8 s): After 1.75 seconds, this hero slams down to the ground, dealing 50 (+4% per level) damage to enemies in the area and landing in [alt-W] form. this hero can begin to cast Abilities 3 seconds after landing.
[alt-E] basic (cooldown 12 s, cost 25): Create a fissure of earth that deals 90 (+4% per level) damage to enemies hit and Stuns them for 0.75 seconds.  [alt-Q]: [E] Lunge in a direction
[alt-W#2] basic (cooldown 6 s, cost 15): After a 1 second delay, deal 30 (+4% per level) damage to all enemies in an area and create a pool of magma that deals 24 (+4% per level) damage per second and Slows enemies by 35%. Lasts 6 seconds.  [alt-Q]: [W] Damage enemies near this hero
