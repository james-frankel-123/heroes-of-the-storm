Hero code: K709
Basic attack (level 1 values; several entries mean alternate forms, units or attack modes): range 5.5, one attack every 0.9 s, 80 damage per attack.

ABILITIES
[Q] basic (cooldown 7 s, cost 50): Launch a rift that deals 170 (+4% per level) damage to enemies in its path. If an enemy Hero is hit, reduce its cooldown by 5 seconds and refund 50 Mana.  Quest: Hit 40 enemy Heroes with [Q] without dying.  Reward: Permanently increase the damage dealt by 75 and cooldown reduction for hitting a Hero by 1 second.
[W] basic (cooldown 9 s, cost 60): Protect an allied Hero from all damage for 1.5 seconds.  Upon expiration, [W] heals the target for 20% of the damage it absorbed.
[E] basic (cooldown 16 s, cost 65): Create a set of portals between this hero and the target location, allowing allies to teleport between both. The portals last 6 seconds.
[R1] heroic (cooldown 40 s, cost 75): Polymorph an enemy Hero for 2 seconds, Silencing them and making them unable to attack. On expiration, [R1] spreads to other nearby enemy Heroes.
[R2] heroic (cooldown 80 s, cost 75): After 0.5 seconds, unleash a wave of energy that places enemy Heroes in Time Stop for 3 seconds.
[D] trait: Instead of mounting, this hero can transform into a raven, increasing Movement Speed by 20%. While transformed, this hero can see and fly over all terrain and is immune to all effects.
[Z] mount (cooldown 4 s): Transform into a raven, increasing Movement Speed by 20%. While transformed, this hero can see and fly over terrain and is immune to all effects.
