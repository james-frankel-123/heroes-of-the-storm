Hero code: K117
Basic attack (level 1 values; several entries mean alternate forms, units or attack modes): range 6.5, one attack every 0.25 s, 14 damage per attack.

ABILITIES
[Q] basic (cooldown 7 s, cost 55): Channel a beam of Psionic energy, dealing 280 (+4% per level) damage and Slowing enemies in a line by 30% for 2 seconds.
[W] basic (cooldown 8 s, cost 60): Create a [W] at the targeted location for 3 seconds. While in a storm, enemies take 38 (+4% per level) damage every 0.5 seconds, increasing by 20% for each consecutive instance of damage, up to 100%.
[E] basic (cooldown 18 s, cost 65): After a 0.5 second delay, create a wall that blocks all units from moving through it for 2 seconds.
[R1] heroic (cooldown 90 s, cost 100): this hero transforms into an [R1], gaining a Shield equal to 25% of his maximum Health and empowering [D]. Lasts for 12 seconds.  [D]: Deals 140 (+4% per level) damage to the target and splashes 70 (+4% per level) damage to nearby enemies. Heroes hit have their Spell Armor reduced by 20 for 2 seconds. [D] is fully charged while [R1] is active.
[R2] heroic (cooldown 60 s, cost 60): After a 0.5 second cast, create a [R2] that travels in a long line. Enemy Heroes within the [R2]'s area are quickly pulled towards its center. Touching the center deals 310 (+4% per level) damage and Stuns the target for 1.25 seconds.
[D] trait: this hero's Basic Attack is a Channeled beam that Slows by 15%. While Channeling, your Basic Attack damage and Mana regeneration increases by 25% a second, to a maximum of 100%. While at full charge, gain an extra 3 Mana every second.  This bonus is lost after 6 seconds of not Channeling.
