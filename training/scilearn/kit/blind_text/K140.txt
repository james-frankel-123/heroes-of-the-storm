Hero code: K140
Basic attack (level 1 values; several entries mean alternate forms, units or attack modes): range 1.5, one attack every 0.8332 s, 150 damage per attack.

ABILITIES
[Q] basic (cooldown 8 s, cost 55): After a 0.5 second delay, enemies in front of this hero take 165 (+4% per level) damage and are silenced for 1.5 seconds.
[W] basic (cooldown 12 s, cost 30): Vector Targeting Create a force, pushing this hero and all enemies hit from the targeted point towards the targeted direction. Deals 48 (+4% per level) damage to enemies.
[E] basic (cooldown 6 s, cost 25): Deal 62 (+4% per level) damage to an enemy and an additional 100% damage to enemies between this hero and the target. Restore 70 (+4% per level) health for each Hero hit.
[R1] heroic (cooldown 45 s, cost 60): After channeling, this hero charges forward dealing 200 (+4% per level) damage to all enemies in his path. Distance is increased based on the amount of time channeled, up to 1.5 seconds.  Issuing a Move order while this is channeling will cancel it at no cost. Taking damage will interrupt the channeling.
[R2] heroic (cooldown 30 s, cost 50): this hero targets an area and channels for 1 second, becoming Protected and Unstoppable. After, if he took damage from an enemy Hero, he sends a shockwave that deals 275 (+4% per level) damage.
[D] trait: this hero's Ability damage and self-healing are increased by 100% against enemy Heroes.  Repeatable Quest: Takedowns increase [D] by 3%, up to 30%. [D] gained from Takedowns is lost on death.
