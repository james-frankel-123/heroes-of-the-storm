Hero code: K989
Basic attack (level 1 values; several entries mean alternate forms, units or attack modes): range 2, one attack every 0.8 s, 115 damage per attack.

ABILITIES
[Q] basic (cooldown 8 s, cost 20): Leap to a target, dealing 130 (+4% per level) damage. If the enemy dies within 1.5 seconds, restore 1 charge and refund 20 Mana.
[W] basic (cooldown 12 s, cost 80): After 1.25 seconds, deal 165 (+4% per level) damage to enemies within the target area, Stunning them for 1 second.
[E] basic (cooldown 10 s, cost 65): Pulls enemies within the target area towards this hero, dealing 25 (+4% per level) damage. After 2.5 seconds, an explosion occurs around this hero, dealing 195 (+4% per level) damage to nearby enemies.
[R1] heroic (cooldown 80 s, cost 75): After 0.5 seconds, summon an term C that rushes forward upon spawning, dealing 250 (+4% per level) damage to the first enemy Hero hit and Stunning them for 0.5 seconds.  The term C's Basic Attacks deal 50% of their damage in an area around their target. Reactivate to retarget the term C.
[R2] heroic (cooldown 90 s, cost 80): Deals 74 (+4% per level) damage per second to nearby enemies. Lasts for 7 seconds.
[D] trait: Gain 10% of damage dealt from Basic Attacks and Abilities as Shields for 6 seconds. Shield amount gained doubled against Heroes.  Current maximum: 1004 (+4% per level)
