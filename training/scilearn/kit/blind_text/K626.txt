Hero code: K626
Basic attack (level 1 values; several entries mean alternate forms, units or attack modes): range 1.3, one attack every 1.1 s, 96 damage per attack.

ABILITIES
[Q] basic (cooldown 8 s, cost 45): Violently slash in the chosen direction, dealing 66 (+4% per level) damage to enemies.  Reactivate to slash up to 2 more times. The third slash Stuns enemies for 0.75 seconds.
[W] basic (cooldown 8 s, cost 35): Desecrate the air, dealing 110 (+4% per level) damage to nearby enemies and gaining 25 Armor for 3 seconds.
[E] basic (cooldown 14 s, cost 70): After 0.75 seconds, gain 50% Movement Speed for 2 seconds. While active, this hero can move through enemy Heroes and put them to Sleep for 2.5 seconds.
[R1] heroic (cooldown 60 s, cost 60): Channel on an enemy Hero for 0.75 seconds, then swap Health percentages with the target over 3 seconds.
[R2] heroic (cooldown 100 s, cost 70): After 1 second, disperse into an Invulnerable swarm of bats for 3 seconds, dealing 120 (+4% per level) damage per second to enemies.  [D] heals for 75% of [R2]'s damage to Heroes.
[D] trait: this hero heals for 45% of damage dealt to enemy Heroes and 10% of damage dealt to non-Heroes.

TALENTS (one is chosen per level)
Level 1:
  [T1.1] modifies W: Each time [W] hits an enemy Hero, gain 75 Physical Armor against the next enemy Hero Basic Attack.  If [T1.1] has no charges, this hero will gain 1 charge after 6 seconds.
  [T1.2] modifies Trait: this hero heals for 30% of Physical Damage dealt, and nearby allied Heroes heal for 15% of Physical Damage dealt.
  [T1.3] modifies Passive: this hero heals for 70 (+4% per level) when damaging a nearby enemy Hero. This can occur once every 6 seconds against each enemy Hero, but is also refreshed when they are Slept by [E].
Level 4:
  [T4.1] modifies W: While [W] is active, this hero heals for 12 (+4% per level) when damage is taken and [D] converts 20% more Hero damage into healing.
  [T4.2] modifies W: [W]'s Armor is increased to 50.
  [T4.3] modifies W: When [W] expires, it explodes again for 68 (+4% per level) damage.  For each enemy Hero hit, gain 5 Mana and reduce the cooldown of [W] by 0.75 seconds.  Does not wake Sleeping targets.
Level 7:
  [T7.1] modifies Q: After this hero hits an enemy Hero with [Q], his next Basic Attack deals 60% more damage and reduces the Armor of enemy Heroes by 15 for 2 seconds.
  [T7.2] modifies Q: Each enemy Hero hit by the first 2 slashes of [Q] causes the final slash to steal 1% of maximum Health from enemy Heroes, up to 4%.  If at least 4 enemy Heroes are hit with the first 2 slashes of [Q], then the final slash Stuns enemies for an additional 0.5 seconds.
  [T7.3] modifies E: After casting [E], deal 33 (+4% per level) damage per second to nearby enemies.  This effect lasts for 5 seconds and Basic Attacks against Heroes refresh its duration.  Does not wake Sleeping targets.
Level 10:
  [R2] modifies Heroic (cooldown 100 s): After 1 second, disperse into an Invulnerable swarm of bats for 3 seconds, dealing 120 (+4% per level) damage per second to enemies.  [D] heals for 75% of [R2]'s damage to Heroes.
  [R1] modifies Heroic (cooldown 60 s): Channel on an enemy Hero for 0.75 seconds, then swap Health percentages with the target over 3 seconds.
Level 13:
  [T13.1] modifies E: Increase [E]'s Sleep duration by 0.75 seconds and its Movement Speed bonus by 10%.
  [T13.2] modifies E: Basic Attacks deal 200% bonus damage to Sleeping enemies.  After [E]'s Sleep ends, targets are Slowed by 30% for 3 seconds.
  [T13.3] modifies Trait: When this hero is healed by a Hero, he gains 1% Movement Speed for 6 seconds, up to 15%.
Level 16:
  [T16.1] modifies W: [W] unleashes a wave of bats in front of this hero, dealing 81 (+4% per level) damage.
  [T16.2] modifies Passive: Basic Attacks against Heroes with a higher Health percentage heal for an additional 50% of damage dealt.  Basic Attacks against Heroes with a lower Health percentage grant 35% Attack Speed for 3 seconds.
  [T16.3] modifies Active (cooldown 60 s): Activate to erupt with blind rage, removing the cooldown and Mana cost of [Q], but losing all vision. Lasts 6 seconds.   Enemy Heroes hit by the final slash of [Q] reduces the cooldown of this Ability by 4 seconds.
Level 20:
  [T20.1] modifies Heroic: Upon expiring, [R2]'s bats seek nearby enemy Heroes, dealing 132 (+4% per level) damage and Sleeping them for 2.5 seconds.
  [T20.2] modifies Heroic: Enemy Heroes near the target suffer 75% of the transferred Health as damage over 3 seconds.  Does not benefit from [D].
  [T20.3] modifies Q: Damage from [Q] and Basic Attacks Slows enemies by 25% for 1.5 seconds, and [D] converts 10% more damage into healing versus Slowed Heroes.
  [T20.4] modifies E: Reduce the cooldown of [E] by 4 seconds.  Enemy Heroes put to Sleep by [E] have their vision greatly reduced during the Sleep and for 2 seconds after.
