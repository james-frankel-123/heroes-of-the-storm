Hero code: K573
Basic attack (level 1 values; several entries mean alternate forms, units or attack modes): range 4.5, one attack every 0.25 s, 50 damage per attack.

ABILITIES
[Q] basic (cooldown 12 s, cost 60): Deal 552 (+4% per level) damage to the target and 276 (+4% per level) damage to nearby targets over 4 seconds. Reactivate to select a new target.  Can move and use Abilities while Channeling.
[W] basic (cooldown 10 s, cost 50): Lob a grenade that deals 256 (+4% per level) damage, knocking enemies away.
[E] basic (cooldown 10 s, cost 50): [T1.2] a short distance.
[R1] heroic (cooldown 100 s, cost 100): Call down a term B to attack nearby enemies, dealing 142 (+4% per level) damage every second. Reactivate to assign a new target. Lasts 22 seconds.
[R2] heroic (cooldown 100 s, cost 100): Call down an term E to pilot. The term E deals increased Damage, has 100% increased Basic Attack range, and uses different Abilities. The term E has 25 Armor and lasts 23 seconds.
[D] trait (cooldown 12 s): Activate to have Basic Attacks against Heroes deal bonus damage equal to 2.5% of their maximum Health. Lasts 3 seconds.
Alternate form or second unit abilities:
[alt-Q] basic (cooldown 7 s): Fire the term E's cannons in a straight line, dealing 196 (+4% per level) damage to everything in the path.
[alt-W] basic (cooldown 7 s): Launches a volley of missiles at target area, dealing 132 (+4% per level) damage and slowing enemy Movement Speed by 30% for 2 seconds.
[alt-E] basic (cooldown 8 s): [T1.2] in target direction.
