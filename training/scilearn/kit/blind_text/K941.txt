Basic attack (level 1 values; several entries mean alternate forms, units or attack modes): range 5.5, one attack every 1 s, 85 damage per attack.

ABILITIES
[Q] basic (cooldown 10 s, cost 70): Shoot a globe of destruction, dealing 176 (+4% per level) damage on impact.  Quest: Hitting a Hero or killing a Minion within 1.5 seconds of being hit by [Q] grants 2 term G.  Reward: Each stack of term G increases the damage of [Q] by 1, up to 400.
[W] basic (cooldown 10 s, cost 25): Spawn a term D that marches forward. Warriors deal 35 (+4% per level) damage per Attack and 18 (+4% per level) damage to nearby enemies every second. Lasts for 10 seconds.  Usable while Channeling [E].
[E] basic (cooldown 6 s, cost 25): Channel a beam of death on an enemy, dealing 128 (+4% per level) damage per second for 2.5 seconds. If the Channel lasts its full duration, deal an extra 340 (+4% per level) damage to the target.  this hero's Movement Speed is reduced by 30% while Channeling.
[R1] heroic (cooldown 100 s, cost 60): Rain a small army of term E down on enemies, dealing 85 (+4% per level) damage per impact. term I deal 39 (+4% per level) damage, have 750 (+4% per level) Health and last up to 10 seconds. When term I die they explode, dealing 40 (+4% per level) damage to nearby enemies, doubled against enemy Heroes.  Usable while Channeling [E].
[R2] heroic (cooldown 20 s, cost 100): Activate to make the next [Q] cost no Mana and deal 50% more damage.  Usable while Channeling [E].
[D] trait (cooldown 60 s): Summon a [D] at any allied Mercenary, Minion, or this hero Demon. The term H will cast term F every 7 seconds, instantly killing an enemy Minion. Lasts 20 seconds.  Usable while Channeling [E].
