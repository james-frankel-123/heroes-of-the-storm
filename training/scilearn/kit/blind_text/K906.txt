Hero code: K906
Basic attack (level 1 values; several entries mean alternate forms, units or attack modes): range 1.5, one attack every 1.1 s, 63 damage per attack.

ABILITIES
[Q] basic (cooldown 4 s, cost 25): Throw a [Q] on the ground that heals the first allied Hero that comes in contact with it for 285 (+4% per level).  Limit 5 active term F.
[W] basic (cooldown 12 s, cost 35): Release the [W]. After 0.5 seconds it explodes, dealing 80 (+4% per level) damage to all enemies in the area and Slowing them by 35% for 2 seconds.
[E] basic (cooldown 16 s, cost 40): Unfurl an enchanted scroll over 2.25 seconds, forming a triangle that deals 150 (+4% per level) damage to enemies inside and Roots them for 1.5 seconds.
[R1] heroic (cooldown 70 s, cost 60): After 1 second, Channel for 3 seconds, putting enemy Heroes in front of this hero to Sleep while Channeling, and for 2 seconds after.  Enemies can only be put to Sleep once per cast, and Sleep's effects end instantly if they take damage.
[R2] heroic (cooldown 30 s, cost 35): Vector Targeting After 1 second, create a twirling tome tornado that travels towards the targeted direction, continually knocking away enemies that come into contact with it. Deals 20 (+4% per level) damage to Heroes hit.
[D] trait: When at least 1 other allied Hero is nearby, this hero gains 10 Armor and his Basic Abilities recharge 50% faster.
