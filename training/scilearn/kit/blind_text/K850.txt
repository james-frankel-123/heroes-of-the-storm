Hero code: K850
Basic attack (level 1 values; several entries mean alternate forms, units or attack modes): range 6.6, one attack every 0.8 s, 83 damage per attack.

ABILITIES
[Q] basic (cooldown 4 s, cost 15): Launch 2 term J that deal 80 (+4% per level) damage to enemies they hit.  Stores up to 2 charges.
[W] basic (cooldown 16 s, cost 75): Summon a term H to attack a single target, dealing 71 (+5% per level) damage per second. Lasts 8 seconds.
[E] basic (cooldown 12 s, cost 50): Bombard target area with a term C for 140 (+4% per level) damage.  The pod spawns 2 term I that deal 30 (+4% per level) damage per second and last for 8 seconds.
[R1] heroic (cooldown 120 s, cost 50): Summon a term G on term M anywhere that this hero has vision. this hero can enter a term G and travel to any other term G by right-clicking near it. While inside a term G, this hero regenerates 10% Health and Mana per second.  Stores up to 2 charges. Maximum of 4 term G at a time.  Passive: term M spreads 15% farther.  Passive: While on term M, each Basic Attack reduces all of this hero's cooldowns by 0.4 seconds.
[R2] heroic (cooldown 80 s, cost 70): Summon a [R2] that devours enemies for 4 seconds. Devoured enemies cannot fight and take 94 (+4% per level) damage per second.  Usable on Unstoppable enemies.
[D] trait (cooldown 18 s): Lay a [D] that generates term M. While on term M, this hero moves 20% faster and her summons move 30% faster. Tumors last 240 seconds and reveal the surrounding area while active.  Stores up to 3 charges.
