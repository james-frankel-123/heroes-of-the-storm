Hero code: K593
Basic attack (level 1 values; several entries mean alternate forms, units or attack modes): range 6.5, one attack every 1.5 s, 198 damage per attack.

ABILITIES
[Q] basic (cooldown 4 s, cost 20): Activate to charge an arrow that deals 291 (+4% per level) damage to the first enemy hit. [Q]'s range increases the longer it is Channeled.
[W] basic (cooldown 8 s, cost 50): Fire an arrow that deals 88 (+4% per level) to the first enemy Hero hit. [W] can collide with terrain and Structures, splitting into 5 arrows that travel extra distance, ricochet up to 4 additional times, and deal 88 (+4% per level) damage each to the first enemy hit.
[E] basic (cooldown 20 s, cost 50): Fire an arrow that grants vision in a large area for 5 seconds. Enemies inside are revealed for 1 second. If [E] lands directly on an enemy, it deals 165 (+4% per level) damage to them and follows them as they move.
[R1] heroic (cooldown 60 s, cost 60): After 1.5 seconds, summon a pair of term A which travel forward, dealing 84 (+4% per level) damage every 0.25 seconds to enemy Heroes in its area.  Enemies in the center take 75% increased damage.
[R2] heroic (cooldown 70 s, cost 90): Fire a missile that travels across the battleground. Explodes upon hitting an enemy Hero, dealing 130 (+4% per level) damage to all nearby enemies and Stunning them for 0.5 seconds.  After traveling a medium distance, the damage is increased to 260 (+4% per level) and the Stun duration to 1.25 seconds.  After traveling a long distance, the damage is increased to 390 (+4% per level) and the Stun duration to 2 seconds.
[D] trait (cooldown 25 s): Jump over unpathable terrain or Structures, up to a maximum range.
