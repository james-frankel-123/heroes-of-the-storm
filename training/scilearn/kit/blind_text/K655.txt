Hero code: K655
Basic attack (level 1 values; several entries mean alternate forms, units or attack modes): range 1, one attack every 0.8 s, 95 damage per attack.

ABILITIES
[Q] basic (cooldown 15 s, cost 75): this hero lashes out his tongue, dealing 160 (+4% per level) damage to the first enemy hit, Stunning and dragging them with him for 1.75 seconds.  If this hero is Stunned or Silenced while using [Q], the effect ends.
[W] basic (cooldown 10 s, cost 50): Deal 52 (+4% per level) damage every 0.5 seconds to nearby enemies for 3.5 seconds. While active, you are able to move through units. Can be cast during [Q] and Burrow.
[E] basic (cooldown 20 s, cost 65): Burrow into the ground, entering Stasis and becoming Invulnerable for 2 seconds. While Burrowed, this hero can move at 50% movement speed.
[R1] heroic (cooldown 60 s, cost 70): Launch biomass that hits the first enemy Hero dealing 200 (+4% per level) damage, revealing, Silencing, and Slowing them 30% for 3 seconds. Additionally, their vision radius is greatly reduced for 6 seconds.
[R2] heroic (cooldown 60 s, cost 70): After 4 seconds, heal for 100% of the damage this hero took over this period.
[D] trait (cooldown 5 s): Heal this hero for 29 (+4% per level) Health per stored term C over 5 seconds. Can be cast during [Q] and Burrow.  Passive: Gain 10 term C from Takedowns and 2 term C from nearby Minions dying. Maximum of 50 term C.
[Z] mount (cooldown 75 s): Activate to burrow to a bush on the Battleground.  Passive: Gain 20% movement speed while in a bush and for 2 seconds after leaving.
