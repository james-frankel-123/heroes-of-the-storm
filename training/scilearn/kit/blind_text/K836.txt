Hero code: K836
Basic attack (level 1 values; several entries mean alternate forms, units or attack modes): range 1, one attack every 0.9 s, 88 damage per attack.

ABILITIES
[Q] basic (cooldown 10 s, cost 45): Throw a hammer, dealing 110 (+4% per level) damage to the first enemy hit and Stunning them for 1.25 seconds.  After reaching level 10, [Q] pierces to hit an additional target. this hero's Basic Attacks reduce the cooldown of [Q] by 1 second and restore 3 Mana.
[W] basic (cooldown 8 s, cost 50): Blast nearby enemies for 96 (+4% per level) damage and Slow them by 30% for 2.5 seconds. Heroes hit also have their Attack Speed reduced by 30% for the duration.
[E] basic (cooldown 10 s, cost 55): Leap to target location, dealing 59 (+4% per level) damage to enemies on landing. Upon leaping, gain 30 Armor for 2 seconds, reducing damage taken by 30%.
[R1] heroic (cooldown 90 s, cost 80): Transform for 20 seconds, gaining 1000 (+4% per level) Health.
[R2] heroic (cooldown 40 s, cost 60): Stun target enemy Hero, and wind up a punch dealing 319 (+4% per level) damage and knocking the target back, hitting enemies in the way for 319 (+4% per level) damage and knocking them aside.
[D] trait: this hero restores 55 (+4% per level) Health per second when he has not taken damage for 4 seconds. When below 40% Health, increased to 111 (+4% per level) Health per second.
