Hero code: K956
Basic attack (level 1 values; several entries mean alternate forms, units or attack modes): range 1.5, one attack every 1.5 s, 261 damage per attack.

ABILITIES
[Q] basic (cooldown 10 s, cost 40): Infest an allied Hero with a [Q] that heals the target for 222 (+4% per level) Health over 4.5 seconds. [Q] can spread to a nearby allied Hero every 0.75 seconds.  Each cast of [Q] can only spread to each allied Hero 1 time.
[W] basic (cooldown 10 s, cost 50): Hurl a pustule that impacts all enemy Heroes in its path, dealing 20 (+4% per level) damage and Slowing by 5%, increasing to 50% over 3 seconds. Deals an additional 88 (+4% per level) damage upon expiring or being removed.
[E] basic (cooldown 10 s, cost 60): Channel at a target location, creating an area that deals 136 (+4% per level) damage per second to non-Structure enemies and Silences them. Deals 50% reduced damage to non-Heroes.  Does not cost Mana while Channeling, and lasts until canceled or interrupted.
[R1] heroic (cooldown 20 s, cost 50): Extend this hero's arm. If it hits an enemy Hero, they are rapidly shoved until they collide with terrain, dealing 190 (+4% per level) damage and Stunning them for 0.5 seconds. this hero gains 50 Armor while shoving an enemy.
[R2] heroic (cooldown 60 s, cost 60): Swipe 3 times in front of this hero over 1.75 seconds, dealing 48 (+4% per level) damage to enemies hit and knocking them away. Each swipe is larger than the previous.
[D] trait (cooldown 16 s): Activate to detonate all of this hero's Viruses. Each [Q] heals its target for 435 (+4% per level) Health, and each [W] does 100 (+4% per level) damage and Slows its target by 70% for 2 seconds.  Can be cast while Channeling [E].
