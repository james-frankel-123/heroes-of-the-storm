Hero code: K683
Basic attack (level 1 values; several entries mean alternate forms, units or attack modes): range 1.5, one attack every 1 s, 148 damage per attack; range 5.5, one attack every 1 s, 148 damage per attack.

ABILITIES
[Q] basic (cooldown 9 s, cost 55): Hurl a flask that deals 55 (+4% per level) damage to the first enemy hit and explodes for 220 (+4% per level) damage to enemies in a cone behind them.  beast form: [alt-Q] Swipe forward, damaging enemies hit.
[W] basic (cooldown 20 s, cost 70): Gain 50% Attack Speed for 3 seconds. Basic Attacks refresh this duration, and reduce the cooldown of [W] by 0.5 seconds.
[E] basic (cooldown 5 s, cost 10): Shapeshift into a beast form and leap at an enemy dealing 88 (+4% per level) damage.  beast form: Disengage Roll away and shapeshift into a ranged form.
[R1] heroic (cooldown 80 s, cost 90): Leap at an enemy Hero and shapeshift into a beast form, slashing for 355 (+4% per level) damage. If this kills them, the Ability can be used a second time within 10 seconds for free.
[R2] heroic (cooldown 30 s, cost 50): this hero shapeshifts into a ranged form and fires a bullet that hits the first enemy Hero in its path, dealing 35% of their current Health in damage.  Does not affect Vehicles.
[D] trait: this hero can use certain Abilities to shapeshift between a ranged form and a beast form.  While ranged form, this hero's Basic Attacks are ranged.  While beast form, this hero gains 10 Armor, and his Basic Attacks become melee but deal 40% more damage.
Alternate form or second unit abilities:
[alt-Q] basic (cooldown 4 s, cost 25): Swipe in the targeted direction, dealing 126 (+4% per level) damage to enemies hit.  ranged form: [Q] Damage the first enemy hit and deal heavy damage behind them.
[alt-W] basic: Gain 50% Attack Speed for 3 seconds. Basic Attacks refresh this duration, and reduce the cooldown of [W] by 0.5 seconds.
[alt-E] basic (cooldown 5 s): Roll away and shapeshift into a ranged form.  ranged form: [E] Leap at an enemy and shapeshift into a beast form.
