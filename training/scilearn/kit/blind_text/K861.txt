Hero code: K861
Basic attack (level 1 values; several entries mean alternate forms, units or attack modes): range 5, one attack every 0.125 s, 23 damage per attack.

ABILITIES
[Q] basic (cooldown 9 s): Dash towards an area.  Stores up to 3 charges.
[W] basic (cooldown 8 s): Deal 220 (+4% per level) damage to a nearby enemy, prioritizing Heroes. Gain 5% [R1] charge when using Melee against an enemy, and 10% against Heroes.
[E] basic (cooldown 20 s): After 1.25 seconds, this hero returns to the position she was at 2 seconds prior to casting, refilling her ammo, and removing all negative status effects from herself.
[R1] heroic: Fire a short range bomb that can attach to an enemy if it hits them. The bomb explodes after 2 seconds dealing 298 (+6% per level) damage to them and 149 (+6% per level) damage to other nearby enemies.  This ability is slowly charged over time by dealing damage to enemies with Basic Attacks and Melee.
[D] trait (cooldown 1 s): this hero can Basic Attack while moving, and after attacking 10 times she needs to reload over 0.75 seconds. this hero can manually reload early by activating Reload.  Basic Attacks heal this hero for 10% of the damage dealt.
