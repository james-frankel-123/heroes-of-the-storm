Hero code: K110
Basic attack (level 1 values; several entries mean alternate forms, units or attack modes): range 1, one attack every 0.7 s, 26 damage per attack.

ABILITIES
[Q] basic (cooldown 4 s): Spawn and attach a [Q] to a target ally or Structure. While active, this hero controls the [Q], gaining access to new Abilities. The [Q] is able to gain XP from nearby enemy deaths.
[W] basic (cooldown 10 s): Spawn a mine that becomes active after a short time. Deals 153 (+4% per level) damage and reveals the enemy for 4 seconds. Lasts 90 seconds.  Stores up to 3 charges.
[R1] heroic (cooldown 90 s): Turn an allied Minion or term I into a term E. When enemy Minions near the term E die, it gains 2% Health and 2% Basic Attack damage, stacking up to 40 times.  The term E can be healed by [alt-E] and has the ability to Burrow to a visible location every 80 seconds.  Using [Q] on the term E allows this hero to control it, in addition to [Q]'s normal benefits.  This Ability can be reactivated to automatically cast [Q] on his term E.
[R2] heroic (cooldown 70 s): Clone target allied Hero and control it for 20 seconds. this hero has perfected the clone, granting it 20% Spell Power, 20% bonus Attack Damage, and 10% bonus Movement Speed. Cannot use their Heroic Ability.
[D] trait (cooldown 15 s): Spawns a term I to attack down the nearest lane every 15 seconds. term I last for 16 seconds, have 350 (+4% per level) health and deal 46 (+4% per level) damage with each Basic Attack, dealing 25% bonus damage to enemy Structures.
[Z] mount (cooldown 30 s): Quickly tunnel to a visible location
Alternate form or second unit abilities:
[alt-E] basic (cooldown 6 s): Shields the assisted ally for 150 (+4% per level). Allied Heroes are healed for 22 (+4% per level) Health per second while the Shield is active. Lasts for 6 seconds.
[alt-Q] basic (cooldown 3 s): Shoots a spike towards target area that deals 119 (+4% per level) damage to the first enemy it contacts.
[alt-W] basic (cooldown 6 s): Deals 120 (+4% per level) damage to nearby enemies.
[alt-Z] mount (cooldown 80 s): Order your term A to quickly tunnel to a visible location
