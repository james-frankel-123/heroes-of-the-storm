Hero code: K875
Basic attack (level 1 values; several entries mean alternate forms, units or attack modes): range 1.25, one attack every 0.55 s, 78 damage per attack.

ABILITIES
[Q] basic (cooldown 6 s): Dive at the target, dealing 66 (+4% per level) damage and flipping to the other side of the target.
[W] basic (cooldown 8 s): Dash towards target point, dealing 119 (+4% per level) damage to enemies along the way. Hitting an enemy increases this hero's Basic Attack damage by 35% for 3 seconds.
[E] basic (cooldown 15 s): Evade enemy Basic Attacks for 2.5 seconds.
[R1] heroic (cooldown 100 s): Charge to target unit, dealing 251 (+4% per level) damage on impact and stunning for 1 second.
[R2] heroic (cooldown 120 s): Transform into term B at the target location, dealing 46 (+4% per level) damage in the area. Temporarily increases maximum Health by 220 (+4% per level) for each Hero hit by the initial impact. Lasts for 18 seconds.
[D] trait: Basic Attacks heal for 30% of damage dealt and reduce Ability cooldowns by 1 second.
