Hero code: K525
Basic attack: ranged (range and rate not available).

ABILITIES
[D] trait (cost 35): Every basic attack against heroes, and every 2 basic attacks against non-heroes generate charge. Once fully charged this hero can activate [D] to unleash a barrage of 6 orb at a target location, each dealing 72 damage on impact. Charge fully decays if this hero does not hit an enemy with a Basic Attack after 6 seconds. Quest: Hit enemy Heroes with [D] to grant 1 stack per hit and increases [D]'s damage by 1%. Lose all stacks upon dying. Reward (20 Stacks): +2 [D] Range Stack loss on death lowered to 6. Reward (60 Stacks): -1 Basic Attack required to charge [D]. Mythic Reward (100 Stacks): +50% Basic Attack and [D] Damage. [D] projectiles Pierce all targets. Stack loss will never cause the Hero to fall below a quest reward threshold.
[Q] basic (cooldown 7 s, cost 45): Shoot a orb that deals 60 damage to enemies hit, and an additional 120 damage to enemies in the center. If an enemy Hero or Structure is hit by the center, they are marked for 4 seconds. Targets afflicted by [Q] are Revealed and draw the summon C and summon A towards them.
[W] basic (cooldown 15 s, cost 50): Summon the summon C, which pulses for 50 damage per second to nearby enemies and lasts 8 seconds. The summon C moves to [Q]'s impact location. The summon C will pursue targets afflicted by [Q] and detonate on impact, dealing 245 damage to nearby enemies and 61 more damage to the Marked target.
[E] basic (cooldown 15 s, cost 50): Cast to teleport this hero 3 times in a triangular pattern. Each time this hero teleports, her Basic Attacks deal 10% more damage while the Ability is active. Reactivate to end [E] early and remain at your current location.
[R1] heroic (cooldown 75 s, cost 80): Channel a powerful orb that grows over time until reaching maximum size after 2 seconds. Recast to launch the Orb early. Enemies that come into contact with the Orb take 90 damage and are Blinded for 1 second. When the Orb reaches the end of its path, it detonates in a large explosion, dealing 400 damage. Charging up this Ability increases its contact damage up to 166 and its explosion damage up to 700. Contact damage is dealt every 0.25 seconds.
[R2] heroic (cooldown 75 s, cost 75): After channeling for 2 seconds, summon a summon B that spawns up to 3 summon A to attack nearby enemies. You can have up to 1 active summon B. Enemies near the summon B are Slowed by 25%. summon A are attracted to targets afflicted by [Q]. The Portal expires after 20 seconds. summon A automatically choose a nearby lane to push. summon A Basic Attacks deal 50 damage per hit. summon A lose Health over 12 seconds.
