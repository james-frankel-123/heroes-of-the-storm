[W] basic: Currently playing term C, increasing the Movement Speed of this hero and nearby allied Heroes by 10%.  Toggle to play term A instead.

[R1] heroic (cooldown 80 s, cost 100): After 1 second, this hero and nearby allied Heroes gain a 1296 (+4% per level) point Shield that rapidly decays over 6 seconds.
[R2] heroic (cooldown 20 s, cost 30): Quickly skate to an allied Hero. Upon arrival the ally is healed for 230 (+4% per level), this hero is healed for 80 (+4% per level), and both gain Unstoppable for 1 second.
[D] trait (cooldown 20 s): While moving alongside terrain, activate to slide towards a targeted location. Enemies hit take 100 (+4% per level) damage and are Slowed by 75% for 1 second.
[Z] mount: When moving alongside terrain, this hero begins to [Z] for 2 seconds. While [Z] is active, this hero can walk through units and gains 20% Movement Speed that stacks with other bonuses.
