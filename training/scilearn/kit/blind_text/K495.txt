Hero code: K495
Basic attack (level 1 values; several entries mean alternate forms, units or attack modes): range 2.25, one attack every 1 s, 100 damage per attack.

ABILITIES
[Q] basic (cooldown 6 s, cost 40): Unleash your sword in the target direction, continuously dealing 30 (+4% per level) damage to enemies caught in its path.
[W] basic (cooldown 8 s, cost 35): Passive: Basic Attack and Ability damage cause enemies to bleed for 44 (+4% per level) damage over 4 seconds. Stacks 5 times.  Active: this hero deals 32 (+4% per level) damage and heals for 85 (+4% per level) Health per enemy Hero affected. Damage and healing is increased by 50% per each additional stack on that Hero.
[E] basic (cooldown 16 s, cost 70): Attach your sword on the first enemy Hero hit, dealing 96 (+4% per level) damage and Stunning them for 0.25 seconds. Once attached, this hero becomes Immune to all effects, swinging around the target for 2.75 seconds and dealing 105 (+4% per level) damage to enemies between you and the target.  Re-activate to send you to the target's location knocking them away, dealing 108 (+4% per level) damage and Stunning them for 0.75 seconds.
[R1] heroic (cooldown 60 s, cost 50): Deal 44 (+4% per level) damage to nearby enemies every 0.5 seconds for 2.5 seconds as your sword grows outward. Upon expiring, deal 160 (+4% per level) damage to nearby enemy Heroes and Stun them for 1.5 seconds.
[R2] heroic (cooldown 40 s, cost 45): After 1 second, deal 395 (+4% per level) damage to enemies in a line. This damage is increased by 25% against enemy Heroes who are below 50% Health.
[D] trait (cooldown 25 s): this hero fires a [D] that pulls her to terrain it contacts. If an enemy Hero is hit, they take 35 (+4% per level) damage and this hero launches at them, kicking them for an additional 108 (+4% per level) damage.  Can be used while [E] is active.
