Hero code: K918
Basic attack (level 1 values; several entries mean alternate forms, units or attack modes): range 2, one attack every 0.6 s, 102 damage per attack.

ABILITIES
[Q] basic (cooldown 14 s): Teleport a short distance in the direction of the mouse cursor, creating 2 [Q] for 8 seconds with 100% of this hero's current Health that Basic Attack enemies for 11 (+4% per level) damage. Images have their duration increased by 1 second each time they or this hero use a Basic Attack against an enemy Hero. Whenever an Image takes damage, it deals that amount of damage to itself, effectively doubling the damage it takes.  Maximum 2 [Q] can be active at at time. Using [Q] removes most negative effects from this hero.
[W] basic (cooldown 10 s): this hero's next Basic Attack within 8 seconds will be a [W], dealing 50% increased damage. This also applies to Images, and does not break [E].  Passive: this hero and his Images deal a [W] on every 4th Basic Attack.
[E] basic (cooldown 15 s): Grant this hero Stealth for up to 10 seconds or until he attacks, uses an Ability, or takes damage.  While Stealthed, this hero heals for 3% of his maximum Health every second, his Movement Speed is increased by 20% and he can pass through other units. Remaining stationary for at least 1.5 seconds while Stealthed grants Invisible.  this hero is Unrevealable for the first 1 second of [E].
[R1] heroic (cooldown 25 s): Cause a [R1] of destructive force around this hero for 3 seconds, granting him 25 Armor and dealing 180 (+4% per level) damage per second to nearby enemies.
[R2] heroic (cooldown 8 s): [Q] can be controlled individually or as a group and their damage is increased by 100%.  Passive: term A's cooldown is reduced to 8 seconds.
[1] activable: [1]  Issue orders to this hero only.
[2] activable: [2]  Issue orders to this hero and all [Q].
