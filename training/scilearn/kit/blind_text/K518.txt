Hero code: K518
Basic attack (level 1 values; several entries mean alternate forms, units or attack modes): range 1.25, one attack every 1.05 s, 105 damage per attack.

ABILITIES
[Q] basic (cooldown 12 s, cost 65): Charge an enemy, knocking them back, dealing 40 (+4% per level) damage and gaining 15% Movement Speed for 2 seconds. If the enemy hits terrain, they are Stunned for 1 second and take an additional 130 (+4% per level) damage.
[W] basic (cooldown 8 s, cost 35): Unleashes fire waves toward the targeted area that deal 12 (+4% per level) damage each. Once they reach maximum range they return, dealing an additional 36 (+4% per level) damage. this hero heals for 130% of the damage dealt to Heroes.
[E] basic (cooldown 12 s, cost 35): Grabs the target and slams it behind this hero, dealing 73 (+4% per level) damage and Stunning for 0.25 seconds.
[R1] heroic (cooldown 90 s, cost 50): Create a demonic rune under each enemy Hero on the battleground. After 1.75 seconds the rune explodes dealing 137 (+4% per level) damage and Stunning them for 1.75 seconds.
[R2] heroic (cooldown 90 s, cost 80): After 0.5 seconds, become Unstoppable and Channel for up to 4 seconds, dealing 50 (+4% per level) damage every 0.25 seconds to enemies in front of this hero. Enemies affected are Slowed by 4% for 2 seconds, up to 40%.  [R2]'s direction changes with your mouse cursor position.
[D] trait: Repeatable Quest: Gain 10 Souls per Hero killed and 1 Soul per Minion, up to 100. For each Soul, gain 0.3% maximum Health. If this hero has 100 Souls upon dying, he will resurrect in 5 seconds but lose 100 Souls.  After reaching level 10, this hero only consumes 75 Souls when resurrecting.
