ABILITIES
[Q] basic (cooldown 12 s, cost 25): Jump to an allied or enemy unit. Enemies are immediately hit with a Basic Attack.  Stores up to 2 charges.
[W] basic (cooldown 10 s, cost 65): Heal nearby Heroes for 285 (+4% per level) and give them 15% Movement Speed for 3 seconds.
[E] basic (cooldown 10 s, cost 45): this hero's next Basic Attack increases his Attack Speed and Attack Range by 100% for 2 seconds.
[R1] heroic (cooldown 50 s, cost 77): Become Invulnerable and strike 7 times over 2 seconds. Each strike hits the highest Health nearby Hero for 7% of their maximum Health.
[R2] heroic (cooldown 50 s, cost 50): Protect an allied Hero from death, causing them to be healed for 1200 (+4% per level) if they take fatal damage in the next 3 seconds.
[D] trait: Choose between [T1.1], [T1.2], and [T1.3] from the Talents panel.
