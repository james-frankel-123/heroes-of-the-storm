Hero code: K554
Basic attack (level 1 values; several entries mean alternate forms, units or attack modes): range 5.5, one attack every 0.9 s, 65 damage per attack.

ABILITIES
[Q] basic (cooldown 7 s, cost 70): After 1 second, deal 345 (+4% per level) damage in an area.  [D] increases the radius by 50%.
[W] basic (cooldown 10 s, cost 50): Deal 126 (+4% per level) damage over 3 seconds to an enemy, then they explode dealing 215 (+4% per level) damage to all nearby enemies.  Other Heroes damaged by this explosion are also affected by [W], though the secondary explosions cannot spread.  [D] makes this Ability cost no Mana and have no cooldown.
[E] basic (cooldown 12 s, cost 90): Stun the first enemy hit for 1 second.  [D] causes [E] to stun the first 3 enemies hit and increases the stun duration by 50%.
[R1] heroic (cooldown 100 s, cost 80): After 1.5 seconds, cast a slow-moving fireball that deals 810 (+5% per level) damage to an enemy Hero and 405 (+5% per level) damage to enemies nearby.
[R2] heroic (cooldown 60 s, cost 80): Launch a [R2] to an area, dealing 84 (+4% per level) damage to enemies along the way. The [R2] persists for 7 seconds, attacking enemies for 84 (+4% per level) damage and splashing for 42 (+4% per level) damage.
[D] trait (cooldown 6 s): Activate to make this hero's next Basic Ability more powerful.
