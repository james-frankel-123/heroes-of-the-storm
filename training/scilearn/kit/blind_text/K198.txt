Hero code: K198
Basic attack (level 1 values; several entries mean alternate forms, units or attack modes): range 2, one attack every 0.9 s, 125 damage per attack; range 4.5, one attack every 0.9 s, 125 damage per attack.

ABILITIES
[Q] basic (cooldown 5 s, cost 20): Hurl a lightning javelin that deals 158 (+4% per level) damage to the first enemy hit and splits into two lightning bolts that deal 158 (+4% per level) damage to enemies in their path.
[W] basic (cooldown 15 s, cost 50): After 0.5 seconds, deal 50 (+4% per level) damage and Blind enemies in the target area for 2 seconds.  Passive: this hero deals 15% increased damage to Blinded targets.
[E] basic (cooldown 10 s, cost 50): Charge at an enemy, and upon arriving Channel for up to 1.5 seconds, dealing 64 (+4% per level) damage to enemies in front of this hero every 0.25 seconds. Deals 50% reduced damage to non-Heroes. Avoidance remains charged while Channeling.
[R1] heroic (cooldown 60 s, cost 60): Throw a ball of lightning at an enemy Hero that bounces up to 6 times between nearby enemy Heroes and this hero, dealing 180 (+4% per level) damage to enemies hit.
[R2] heroic (cooldown 60 s, cost 60): Summon a [R2] that rushes to this hero after 0.75 seconds, pulling the first enemy Hero hit, dealing 225 (+4% per level) damage and Stunning them for 0.5 seconds at the end of her path. The [R2] knocks back all other enemy Heroes in her way.
[D] trait: While moving, this hero gradually gains up to 30 Armor over 3 seconds. After being stationary for 0.75 seconds, the Armor is rapidly lost over .5 seconds.  Avoidance remains charged while Fending.
