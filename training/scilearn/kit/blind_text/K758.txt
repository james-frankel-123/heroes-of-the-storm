Hero code: K758
Basic attack (level 1 values; several entries mean alternate forms, units or attack modes): range 5.5, one attack every 1 s, 60 damage per attack.

ABILITIES
[Q] basic (cooldown 1.5 s, cost 75): Release a wave of flame, dealing 210 (+4.5% per level) damage to enemies.
[W] basic (cooldown 10 s): Drain the life from an enemy over 3 seconds, dealing 132 (+4% per level) damage per second and healing this hero for 188 (+4% per level) Health per second.
[E] basic (cooldown 14 s, cost 80): Call forth three bursts of shadow energy, dealing 204 (+4.5% per level) damage over 6 seconds. [E] can stack up to 3 times on an enemy.
[R1] heroic (cooldown 80 s, cost 90): After 0.5 seconds, deal 120 (+4% per level) damage to enemy Heroes in an area and Fear them for 2 seconds. While Feared, Heroes are Silenced and are forced to run away from [R1]'s center.
[R2] heroic (cooldown 70 s, cost 80): After 1.5 seconds, summon a rain of meteors in an area for 7 seconds. Each meteor deals 165 (+4% per level) damage in a small area.
[D] trait: this hero does not regenerate Mana.  Activate to restore 25% of this hero's Mana.
