Hero code: K603
Basic attack (level 1 values; several entries mean alternate forms, units or attack modes): range 1.5, one attack every 1.3 s, 155 damage per attack.

ABILITIES
[Q] basic (cooldown 6 s, cost 65): Unleash holy energy around this hero, dealing 42 (+4% per level) damage to nearby enemies and healing her for 96 (+4% per level).  Charging up this Ability increases its damage up to 140 (+4% per level), and healing up to 320 (+4% per level).
[W] basic (cooldown 6 s, cost 65): Swing this hero's hammer, dealing 38 (+4% per level) damage to enemies in front of her and knocking them away.  Charging up this Ability increases its knockback distance, and damage up to 125 (+4% per level). Enemies hit at maximum charge are Stunned for 0.75 seconds.
[E] basic (cooldown 6 s, cost 65): Leap to a location, dealing 225 (+4% per level) damage to enemies in an area and Slowing them by 50% for 1 second.  Charging up this Ability increases its range.
[R1] heroic (cooldown 120 s, cost 80): Surround this hero in a barrier for 3 seconds, absorbing all damage taken and healing her for 50% of the damage received.
[R2] heroic (cooldown 40 s, cost 40): this hero sanctifies the ground around her, gaining 50 Armor until she leaves the area.
[D] trait (cooldown 8 s): Activate to instantly charge this hero's next Basic Ability at no mana cost. [D]'s cooldown is reset when this hero casts a Heroic ability.  Passive: this hero's Basic Abilities charge up over 1.5 seconds, increasing in effectiveness, but reducing this hero's Movement Speed by 25%.
