ABILITIES
[Q] basic (cooldown 12 s): Activate to begin charging [Q]. Activate again to dash forward, knocking aside enemies and dealing 46 (+4% per level) damage. The dash range increases by up to 250%, depending on how long it is charged.
[W] basic (cooldown 12 s): Ignite nearby enemies, dealing 150 (+4% per level) damage over 5 seconds. Basic Attacking burning enemies re-Ignites them. this hero is healed for 40 (+4% per level) when an enemy is Ignited.
[E] basic (cooldown 8 s): Roll a bomb dealing 91 (+4% per level) damage to enemies in its path. the partner hero can use term A to detonate it to deal 210 (+4% per level) damage in an area.
[R1] heroic (cooldown 40 s): After 1 second, pull enemies towards this hero, Slowing them by 25% for 3 seconds and dealing 175 (+4% per level) damage.
[R2] heroic (cooldown 15 s): Activate to swing the [R2], dealing 150 (+4.5% per level) damage, pushing enemies away, and Stunning them for 0.75 seconds.  Passive: this hero's Basic Attacks deal 25% increased damage.
[D] trait (cooldown 5 s): Activate to gain 25 Armor, but reduce the partner hero's damage by 25%.
