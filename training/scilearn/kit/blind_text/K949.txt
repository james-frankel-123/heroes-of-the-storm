Hero code: K949
Basic attack (level 1 values; several entries mean alternate forms, units or attack modes): range 1.5, one attack every 0.8 s, 84 damage per attack.

ABILITIES
[Q] basic (cooldown 13 s): Throw out a spear that pulls this hero to the first enemy hit, dealing 173 (+4% per level) damage and briefly stunning them. If this hits an enemy, generate 40 Fury.
[W] basic (cooldown 1 s, cost 25): Deals 176 (+4% per level) damage to the target enemy, and 44 (+4% per level) to enemies behind the target.
[E] basic (cooldown 5 s, cost 40): Deals 138 (+4% per level) damage a second to nearby enemies for 3 seconds, healing for 25% of damage dealt. Healing tripled versus Heroes.
[R1] heroic (cooldown 60 s): Leap into the air, dealing 135 (+4% per level) damage to nearby enemies, and stunning them for 1.25 seconds.
[R2] heroic (cooldown 60 s): Increase damage dealt by 40%. Reduce the duration of Stuns, Roots, and Slows against this hero by 50%. Lasts 15 seconds, and extends by 1 second for every 10 Fury gained.
[D] trait: Use Fury instead of Mana, which is gained by taking damage or dealing Basic Attack damage. Using a Basic or Heroic Ability grants 10% Movement Speed for 4 seconds.
