## Notes

- N is the population MLP only (no P3 personal layer). Its weights stop at 2.55.14.95918, so every tranche here is out of time for it. P1 site enriched is in sample on Dpre (see `baselines_neural.md`).
- A0, K0 and E are fit by the harness on the remote workers. "S" fits score Dpre, G and Dpost; "S+Dpre+G" fits score Dpost only. Fits never read a scored row (checked by `evaluator.check_disjoint`).
- **A0 and K0 use player histories; N does not.** Both carry an as-of player-hero experience term (log of the decayed count of the player's earlier games on that hero, 180-day half-life, private accounts excluded). Zeroing single terms of A0 (fit S+Dpre+G) on Dpost, without a refit, raises log loss by: hero strength by build +0.0061, experience +0.0052, counter pairs +0.0035, hero x map +0.0024, ally pairs +0.0017, hero x tier +0.0009, no-healer +0.0003. So a draft-only A0 sits near 0.6777, about 0.001 better than N, and most of A0's lead over N is personal information N never sees. The fair personal comparison is N plus P3 (59.70% in FRAMING 1), which is not scored here. In K0 the experience term is worth +0.0052 too, and the kit-axis shapes and map-class products together add about 0.0025 over hero strength alone (range +0.0015, map class x siege +0.0011).
- A0's lead over N also holds when A0 is fit on S only, the same cutoff as N's weights (Dpost: -0.0046, SE 0.00035).
- O1 (2.57), O4 (Xal'atath) and the C-based comparisons wait for the day-14 C batch. O3 (leave one hero out) and the S rolling-origin backtests are not run yet.
