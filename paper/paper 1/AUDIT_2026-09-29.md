## Summary

Both drift-paper failure modes appear in paper 1, and each of them touches a headline claim.

1. **Leakage.** The aggregate-statistic features contain the outcomes of the test games.
   - Removing each test game's own contribution drops the enriched model's held-out accuracy from 58.07% to 57.04%. That is below the naive model (57.80%).
   - A sham control that removes 38,981 random *train* games instead changes nothing (58.09%).
   - The enriched model's accuracy edge, the 4,100-config sweep marginals, part of the NGS "degradation", and about 30% of the supplement's metric-validity spread are all produced by the test games' own outcomes.
2. **Self-grading and confounded arms.** The +0.023 "value of domain features" (B_fullwp vs K_truebase) exists only under judges that carry the enriched features.
   - Table VI's judge is the exact checkpoint B was trained against.
   - Under the naive, hero-strength, QM-2021 and QM-2026 judges, the gap is +0.002 to -0.022.
   - The "constrained beats unconstrained MCTS" head-to-head compared two different checkpoints.
   - Against matched opponents, the mask adds nothing (0.697 vs 0.700 consensus).

On the author's overfitting hypothesis, the evidence points the same way. The enriched value function learned to rely on pairwise features whose train-time correlation with the label is inflated by self-inclusion (point 1 above). MCTS then optimizes that function. Two of the four tournament judges are contestants' own value functions, and a third shares the same statistics. Deeper search raises the self-judged score by +0.041 but the QM-2021 judge's score by only +0.002 (see A1 and A2).

## (A) Could change a headline claim or an accept decision

### A1. Test-set outcomes are inside the enriched features. Removing them erases the enriched accuracy edge.

**Lines:**
- 43: 56-58% accuracy.
- 53: "57.7--58.1%".
- 93: "Train/test splits are at the replay level throughout".
- 105-107: Table I.
- 114: aggregates are "a second data source, distinct from our replay corpus".
- 62: "a corpus separate from and larger than our drafts".
- 177: sweep marginals.
- 195: "the test set remains pure".
- 405 and supplement §NGS: 58.1 → 55.5.

**Evidence: how the stats are built.**
- The split is a replay-level random 2% (`shared.split_data`, seed 42; 38,981 test replays; `rerun2026/feature_cache/split_meta.json`).
- Features come from `training/frozen_stats_2026-05-19.json`: Heroes Profile Storm League aggregates for patch 2.55 (`sync/run-once.ts`, `api-client.getHeroMatchups`), pulled at the end of the corpus window.
- Our replays come from the same Heroes Profile replay database. Per hero and tier, our corpus holds a median of 66% (high), 51% (mid) and 38% (low) of the games in the aggregates (`leak_quantify.py` coverage block).
- So each test game's own outcome sits in the hero, hero-map and pairwise win rates its features are computed from. Pairwise cells are admitted at ≥30 games and hero-map cells at ≥50, which gives a single game a lot of weight.
- The claim in line 114 that the aggregates are "distinct from our replay corpus" is false. They are a superset that contains it.

**Evidence: what the leakage is worth.** I subtracted the 38,981 test games' own wins and games from each cell (leave-test-out), re-extracted the test features, and scored the released checkpoints:

| Model | Reported acc. | Leave-test-out acc. | Sham control (random 38,981 train games removed) |
|---|---|---|---|
| naive (197d) | 57.80 | 57.80 | 57.80 |
| hero-strength | 57.70 | 57.69 | 57.71 |
| **enriched 256** | **58.07** | **57.04** | 58.09 |
| enriched 512 | 58.04 | 57.04 | n/a |
| aug_v2 512 (tournament judge) | 57.92 | 56.96 | 57.89 |
| relational 256 | 58.01 | 57.03 | 57.99 |
| absolute 256 (no pairwise) | 57.89 | 57.71 | 57.89 |

- The loss sits in the pairwise and hero-map groups. Hero-level cells are large enough that self-inclusion does not matter there.
- Log-loss rises from 0.6729 to 0.6778 (naive: 0.6742).
- The model was trained on features that contained each training row's own label. It learned to over-trust the pairwise features. On any state whose outcome is not in the stats (every MCTS leaf, every tournament draft, every NGS game), that trust is miscalibrated.
- This is the train/serve skew the drift paper calls "leaky-frozen", and it is the most concrete mechanism behind the suspected WP overfit.

**Consequences:**
- "Enriched 58.1%" should read about 57.0%, which is 0.8pp *below* naive. The 0.3pp accuracy ordering of Table I reverses.
- The 4,100-config sweep marginals (line 177: pairwise +0.20%, largest) are very likely leakage. The pairwise and counter-detail groups carry the self-inclusion.
- NGS (supplement): NGS games are not in the Storm League aggregates.
  - The enriched model's drop from 58.1% on ladder to 55.5% on NGS is really from about 57.0% leak-free, so about 1.5pp.
  - The naive model drops from 57.8% to 55.3%.
  - "Modest degradation under distribution shift" is right, but the enriched-vs-naive comparison flips.
- The paper's thesis, "accuracy does not discriminate", actually gets *stronger*. The sentence "the enriched features... 58.1%" does not survive.

**Fix:**
1. Rebuild the stats from train-split games only. Our corpus has everything needed (hero, hero-map and pairwise counts), and this also makes the stats exactly reproducible (see C5).
   - Alternatively, compute leave-one-out stats per training row.
   - Retrain the Table I models and re-report.
   - This is cheap: phase0 and phase1 WP jobs only.
2. Until that is done, report the leave-test-out numbers above and remove the claim that the aggregates are a distinct corpus (lines 62, 114).
3. The drift paper already uses a time-split, train-only statistics design. Cite it as the fix.

### A2. The +0.023 "enriched features contribute" gap (lines 63, 346, 403) is a self-grading artifact.

**Evidence: who judges Table VI.**
- `phase4_mcts.py` lines 311 and 465 score every configuration's drafts with `wp_enriched_256.pt`.
- That is the exact reward and leaf evaluator that B, C, D, E, F, I and J were trained and searched against (`WP_CHECKPOINTS["enriched_full"]`).
- K_truebase was trained against `wp_true_base.pt` and graded by B's reward model.
- So the arms differ in two factors: the value-function features, and whether the judge is the arm's own objective. This is the confounded-arms pattern from the drift audit.

**Evidence: re-scoring with other judges.** I re-scored the existing per-draft dumps (`rerun2026/results/diversity/*__base.json`; 15 seeds × 200 drafts, mid tier). They reproduce the Table VI Avg WP exactly under the enriched judge. The other judges give:

| Contrast | Enriched judge (self, Table VI) | Aug-512 | Naive SL | Hero-str SL | QM-2021 | QM-2026 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| B_fullwp − K_truebase | **+0.023 (t=5.0)** | +0.023 (4.4) | −0.006 (−1.5) | +0.002 (0.4) | **−0.022 (−2.7)** | **−0.008 (−3.1)** |
| M2_relational − K | +0.016 (2.3) | +0.014 | −0.011 | −0.003 | −0.019 (−2.1) | −0.015 (−3.6) |
| N2_absolute − K | +0.010 (2.7) | +0.016 | +0.004 | +0.008 | −0.008 | −0.008 (−3.2) |
| J_800sim − B (sim scaling) | +0.041 (11.2) | +0.038 | +0.021 (6.2) | +0.028 (6.8) | +0.002 (0.2) | +0.010 (4.1) |
| E_1M − B (training length) | +0.026 (6.4) | +0.024 | +0.016 (4.4) | +0.021 (5.0) | +0.018 (2.2) | +0.010 (3.6) |

- Under both QM judges, which share nothing with either arm, the featureless agent K_truebase is *better* than B_fullwp.
- The relational/absolute decomposition in line 346 also reverses under independent judges.
- Simulation scaling survives only partially. It is half as large under the SL naive judge, a quarter under QM-2026, and zero under QM-2021, where the ordering is B .734, F .745, I .732, J .735 and is not monotone.
- Training length (E_1M) is the one MCTS effect that survives every judge.
- This fits the author's suspicion. Deeper search mostly finds drafts the enriched value function overrates. It is reward-model overoptimization in the sense of the paper's own reference [gao2023].

**What survives:** the structural gap. K_truebase has 20.1% degenerate compositions against 11.0% for B. That metric is rule-based, not judge-based.

**Fix:**
1. Report Table VI Avg WP under at least one judge independent of every arm: QM-2026, or an SL judge trained on a disjoint time window with train-only stats. Keep the self-judged column only as a training diagnostic.
2. Replace "+0.023 WP, p < 0.001" (lines 63, 346, 403) with the structural result. Features buy safety, not independent-judge win probability.
3. Add K_truebase (and ideally J-at-200-sims) to the round-robin.
4. Reword the sim-scaling paragraph (line 340) with the independent-judge magnitudes.

### A3. "Constrained MCTS beats unconstrained MCTS head-to-head" compares two different agents. At matched checkpoints the mask adds nothing.

- The same bug affects `results/iql_tournament.json` and `ns/sept2026` (fork finding; the iql file notes it).

**Evidence: the matched comparison.** Against the same 8 opponents, both orderings, from existing pair files:

| Opponents: the same 8 | Consensus | Naive | Hero-str. | Enriched | Augmented |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Unconstrained J_800sim_s9 | 0.6995 | 0.679 | 0.696 | 0.718 | 0.705 |

3. State that the tournament agents are distilled policies with no search, or add a searched variant.
4. Until then, drop "constrained beats its unconstrained counterpart head-to-head" and "wins all 20". What the mask provides is structural validity at no measured cost. That is a real and publishable result.

### A4. "The lead is not self-graded" (lines 59, 356, 405) is not supported as written.

**Evidence: the judges are contestants' own value functions.** The tournament judges (`phase3b_roundrobin.py:60-64`) are:
- `wp_enriched_256`: the value function of enriched greedy and constrained greedy, and the training and search reward of J_800sim.
- `wp_aug_v2_512`: the value function of enriched+aug greedy.
- So two of the four judges are contestants' own objectives. "No single model's judgment decides the outcome" (line 354) understates this.

**Evidence: the hero-strength judge shares features with the value function.**
- The claim "share *no* features with the underlying value function beyond hero identity" is false for the hero-strength judge.
- Its groups are `hero_wr, team_avg_wr` (`train_jobs.py WP_GROUP_PRESETS`). `team_avg_wr` is also an enriched group, and both read the same leaky `frozen_stats` file.
- Only the naive judge qualifies.

**Evidence: judge-specific inflation.** Own judge minus naive judge:

| Strategy | Own judge | Naive judge | Inflation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Enriched greedy | 0.625 | 0.569 | +0.056 |
| Enriched+aug greedy | 0.601 (aug judge) | 0.558 | +0.043 |
| Gourdeau estimator | 0.502 (enriched judge) | 0.554 | n/a |

- The Gourdeau estimator runs value search with no stats features. Under the naive judge it trails enriched greedy by only 0.015; under the enriched judge by 0.123.

**Evidence: the QM judges.**
- **The Spearman 0.92 has a key-mismatch bug.** `qm2026/train_qm_wp.py:53` defines CONSENSUS_ORDER with keys `gourdeau_est` and `mcq`, but the standings use `gourdeau` and `mcq_t0.5`. `common = [s for s in CONSENSUS_ORDER if s in standings]` therefore silently drops the two strategies, and 0.9167 is computed on 9 of the 11. Recomputed on all 11 (paper's consensus values, ties averaged): 0.907 (QM-2021) and 0.897 (QM-2026).
- **The QM judges do not reproduce the ordering among value-search strategies, which is where the paper's claims live.**
  - Both QM judges put the Gourdeau estimator third (0.552, 0.550), above enriched greedy, constrained greedy and enriched+aug. The consensus puts it sixth.
  - QM-2026 swaps the top two.
  - Spearman over the top 6 strategies is 0.66 (QM-2021) and 0.54 (QM-2026).
  - The 0.9 overall comes from the easy separations: MCQ, then the anchored cluster, then everything that searches.
- So "the ordering they induce is the consensus ordering" (line 358) is false.

**Fix:**
- Rephrase: "The naive judge shares only hero identity with the agents. Two of four judges are contestants' value functions."
- Report per-judge standings in a table.
- Fix the key bug and report all-11 and top-6 rank correlations.
- State plainly that the out-of-corpus judges confirm "search beats anchoring" and "MCTS beats greedy", but not "enriched greedy beats featureless greedy" or "constrained beats unconstrained".

## (C) Reviewer-bait

1. **Test-set model selection.** There is no validation split. `retrain_frozen_stats.train_wp_model` early-stops on test loss, and `cmd_wp` keeps the best of 3 seeds by test accuracy. Every accuracy in Table I, Table V and the sweep is test-selected. At n = 38,981, the accuracy SE is 0.25pp, which is the size of every accuracy difference the paper discusses. Add a validation split.
2. **Inconsistent seed selection across arms.** `wp_enriched_256` is best of 3 (seed 777). `wp_true_base` is a single seed, 42, which is the worst naive seed (57.60 vs 57.80). `wp_aug_v2_256` is seed 42. Another small confound in B vs K and B vs H.
3. **Table V seeds.** Table V's "5 seeds × 1000" are evaluation seeds of one trained model (MANIFEST says so; the paper does not). Differences of 14.0 vs 15.6 vs 17.1% degenerate are within training-seed noise that was never measured.
4. **Omitted configurations.** Table VI shows 11 of 13 configurations. The omitted A_partial (0.708) and G_base (0.676, *below* K_truebase) are not explained, yet the supplement's dual-snapshot table lists them.
5. **Ensemble "data half" diversity.** It is weaker than it looks: all 20 members read the same full-corpus stats, so the halves share the pairwise features and their leaked labels.
6. **Line 241/276 (fork): changed reference model.** "Quadrupling the data tightened the collapse" (59-65 → 69-74% GD agreement) compares against different GD reference models. The 473K-era GD differs from the rerun GD.
7. **Line 129: circularity paragraph.** It names only synergy and counter. It should also name: Avg WP judged by the training reward (A2); the augmented judge trained on stats-derived labels (B3); and the stats containing test outcomes (A1).
8. **Line 358: "a corpus the agent has never touched".** True for the replays. The QM judges' reach beyond composition structure is limited, though: they reorder all value-search methods (A4).
9. **Anonymous repository URL.** `\repourl` points to `https://anonymous.4open.science/status/moba-draft-policies`. `/status/` is the processing-status page; the reviewer link normally has the form `/r/<id>`. Verify it resolves to the repo, and set expiry to "forever" (notes.md already asks).
10. **Stale NGS summary (fork).** `training/ngs2026/NGS_SUMMARY.md` says NGS is "less meta-concentrated", contradicting the supplement's "more". Fix it before it is quoted or released.
