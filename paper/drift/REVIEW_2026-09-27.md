# Pre-submission review: drift paper (draft.tex as of 2026-07-28)

All numbers were traced to artifacts in `training/drift2026/` (results/*.md, *.json, scripts). Line numbers refer to `overleaf/draft.tex`.

Most numbers match: Table 1 cells, the edge framing, Table 3 regret, the detector loop, the dose table, the opponent-model table, the decay curves, and 57.22/57.08. The problems below are ordered by how much damage they would do in review.

## A. Blockers (these change headline claims)

### A1. The maintained agent's statistics include most of the scoring period
`phase_w4_mcts.cell_stats_build("d2c_cumprev")` returns `builds[-2]` = 2.55.16.96881, and `eval_policy_w2b.py` does the same. The maintained drafter's cumulative statistics therefore cover 6 of the 7 post-cutoff builds, about 131K of the ~144K games. The judge-free truth (`future_truth_stats`) is built from all 7 of those builds. The stale agents' statistics stop at or before the cutoff.

So the maintained side drafts using statistics that partly *are* the answer key. In the cumulative pool this is diluted (~7% of the pool), but it points exactly in the direction of the measured effect. For `maintained-d90`, 90-day decay puts most of the weight on those same scoring games. Everything built on judge-free head-to-head scoring is affected: Table 2, the synergy/timing split, the 22.0 vs 36.9 degeneracy comparison (degeneracy itself needs no statistics, but the drafter's choices do), and C2 of the pre-registration.

Two ways to make it clean:
- **Per-build protocol:** drafts for build k use statistics through k-1 and are scored on build k truth only. This is what a real deployment does.
- **Out-of-sample truth:** score the existing drafts against builds that neither agent's statistics touched. The DB now holds a lot of such data: the post-snapshot part of 2.55.16.97039 plus 2.55.17.97605/97650/97771/98025 (about 150K+ games, all June to September). The catch is that this uses up the pre-registered C2 test on data that already exists (see B1). It is still the strongest fix available and probably the right call.

### A2. Staleness is confounded with training recipe
The stale-1yr and stale-2yr agents are trained on the Table 1 row 1 recipe (all history, features frozen at their own cutoff; `w7_staleness.py`). `stale-4mo` is `d2b_allhist`, and its cutoff is the *same* as maintained's. The 4-month contrast is therefore a pure recipe contrast (era-aligned statistics during training versus frozen), plus the A1 leak. It is not four months of staleness. Line 520 says the cutoff is "four months before the evaluation window", but it is the start of the window.

The paper itself says (L355) that most of maintained's edge is "baked in at training time." That makes the dose-response shape (0.80 at 4 months, same at 1 year) look like a recipe offset plus a small staleness slope. The W2c replay gives the only decomposition we have: frozen-feature recipe regret +1.682, same maintained-recipe model with never-refreshed statistics +1.063. That is roughly 0.62pp recipe and 0.33pp staleness, at the accuracy level.

Fix: an agent-level control. Train the maintained recipe to each earlier cutoff and freeze its statistics there. That agent is the true "stale" version of maintained. Without it, reword Table 2 as "maintained vs. unmaintained-recipe agents frozen at cutoff X" and move the staleness gradient claim to the decomposition above.

### A3. "The two-year deficit is pure hero timing" (abstract, L122, L561-566) overstates
In the W7 matchups against `maintained`, the 2-year stale agent still drafts 7.9pp more degenerate teams (27.6 vs 19.7, z = -1.1, not significant). It is also significantly *better* on counter quality (-0.67 ± 0.21, z = -3.2). Counters are defined at L201 but never reported, and the counter delta is negative in every matchup (volume-matched z = -5.9). The volume-matched control's synergy is -0.63 ± 0.40, against maintained. That contradicts "The three agree here" (L580).

"The degenerate rate is flat across staleness levels" (L573) comes from `W8B_CHAMPION.md`, where the maintained side is `maintained-d90`. The paper's own maintained matchups are 14.5/35.3, 20.0/28.4, and 19.7/27.6.

Fix: report counters. Restate the 2-year finding as "the deficit is concentrated in hero timing; synergy is intact; the stale agent counters slightly better." Source the flat-degeneracy claim correctly or drop it.

### A4. The pre-registration is not registered
L750 says "we registered three forward claims ... and committed the registration publicly." `paper/drift/` is untracked in git. `PREREG_FORWARD.md` has both sign-off boxes unchecked. The public repo `maxsegan/moba-draft-policies` (last push 2026-08-09) contains nothing from the drift work, and L771 still carries the `<DATE/COMMIT>` placeholder.

Also, four new builds have shipped since the snapshot (2.55.17.97605 on 7/20, 97650, 97771, and 98025 on 9/12). Claims registered today cover only builds after today's commit. The next build will probably land in about 4-8 weeks.

Fix: sign off, commit and push `PREREG_FORWARD.md` (with frozen code hashes) before submission, then fill in the date and commit. Say explicitly that builds shipped between the snapshot and registration are excluded from the registered claims (or reported descriptively). Note too that the old HP API dies 2027-01-01, so the revision-window data depends on the v2 migration.

## B. Wrong numbers and false sentences (easy fixes)

1. **L288-289 "That factor of 2.5 survives deep search: 17.6% vs 9.3%"**: that ratio is 1.9x. It is also a different protocol (W4 benchmark vs future-meta opponent models, 5 seeds) from the 14.5/22.0 head-to-head numbers at L372 and L569. Say "roughly halves under deep search too (17.6 vs 9.3)" and name the protocols so readers don't try to reconcile 9.3, 14.5 and 22.0.
2. **L298 "selecting the most accurate standard regime hands you the most degenerate one"**: false. The most accurate is the patch embedding (56.55, 47.0%), which is 5th of 7 on degeneracy. The most degenerate is decay-90d (50.1%). r = +0.78 and the slope of 16 are correct. Replace with a statement of the correlation.
3. **L250/L262 validation accuracies**: window-12 has the best validation accuracy (59.4). All-history (59.11) is also below the patch embedding (59.14). Move the bold, and change "all-history training is the best regime" to "among the best."
4. **L131 "0.42-0.43 (2021- and 2022-era judges)"**: those judges actually span 0.416-0.469 (ranked 2022: 0.469, 0.461; QM: 0.434, 0.416).
5. **L602 "monotone in judge era"**: not strictly (2022Q1 0.469 > 2022-07 0.461; 2024 0.504 > 2025 0.495). Say "rises with judge era."
6. **L596 "each on a volume-matched window"**: the 2022Q1 judge had 158,709 games against a 314,761 cap. Disclose it.
7. **L606-608 "every judge from 2024 on agrees with reality"**: under the per-draft SE used to call 2023 (0.493) a reversal, 2025-07 (0.495, z = -3.1) and QM-2024 (0.494, z = -2.4) are also below 0.5. Under crossed-seed SE (~0.011), none of the 0.49x judges differ from 0.5. Pick one SE, apply it everywhere, and state the 2023 judge as "at parity."
8. **L628 "the same aged judges reproduce ... at rank correlation 0.92"**: measured only for QM-2021 and QM-2026 (0.917). Say which judges.
9. **L478 "6,500 to 12,000 games depending on tier"**: the tier medians are low 6,460, mid 8,668, high 7,753, so the range is 6,500-8,700. The 12,200 is the pooled p75. L432-434 omits the high tier; add it. The same error is in COAUTHOR_SUMMARY.
10. **Detector units (abstract, L109, L464-466)**: precision 0.79 = 46/58 per (boundary × hero) detection at q = 0.05. Pick/ban recall 0.68 / precision 0.32 are in the same unit. Boundary-level precision is 16/17 = 0.94, which is the stronger number for the maintenance story. State the unit wherever 0.79 appears.
11. **Median latency 9,400 (L110, L472)**: computed on the uncorrected per-hero p < 0.05 test's true positives (n = 98), not on the FDR q = 0.05 detector the paper describes. The median over all WR-flagged heroes is 12,922. Recompute it on the actual detector, or label it.
12. **"Misses were individually small" (L506, L758)**: the missed boundaries 2.55.4.91418 and 2.55.4.91769 list 88 and 64 changed heroes. Soften this, or check whether those were talent-only changes.
13. **L680 "largest policy-cell movement of 2.9pp"**: true for `d2c_cumprev` only. `d2b_allhist` moved healer rate 5.8pp and degeneracy 3.5pp. If the trigger was pre-registered on cumprev, say so, since a reader seeing 5.8 > 3.0 will ask.
14. **L671 "12.18 down to 10.61"**: the first build is 13.12; 12.18 is the second.
15. **L765 "one build every five to six weeks"**: holds only when hotfix builds count. Sizable builds come about every 6.5 weeks. Since May the cadence has been 4 builds in about 10 weeks.
16. **L474, L762 "about ten thousand new ranked games per week"**: the DB now shows about 14-16K SL games per game-date week from April through September 2026. The 10K figure (prereg, measured 7/14) was likely undercounted because of upload lag. Detection latency is therefore closer to 4-5 days. This is good news, and it helps the prospective power argument.
17. **L381 "+0.7 to +0.8pp"**: leaves out the 2-year +0.96.
18. **L558 "volume explains about a fifth"**: compares accuracies measured on different future windows (stale-2yr has its own window). Qualify this or drop the number.
19. **L122 "retains 86%"**: 0.82/0.96 = 85%. Fine if it comes from unrounded values; check.
20. **Win-rate wording**: L381 "+0.7 to +0.8pp of real win rate" and the conclusion's "loses about 0.8 points of real future-meta win rate" read as team win probability. The metric is the mean realized win rate of picked heroes. The abstract words it correctly; match that everywhere.

## Resolution (2026-09-27, same day)

- **A1 (leak): fixed by rescoring.** `training/drift2026/w12_clean_truth.py` scores every head-to-head file against 298,428 games from 2.55.16.97039 (full build) plus the four 2.55.17 builds. No agent's statistics touch these games. The original truth reproduces exactly. Results are in `results/W12_CLEAN_TRUTH.md`, and the paper now uses them throughout.
- **A2 (recipe vs staleness): clarified.** `stale-4mo` is renamed `unmaintained` (the macro changed), and its definition states the shared cutoff. Section 7 explains that row 1 prices maintenance (recipe plus refresh) and that row differences price staleness. The two-thirds/one-third accuracy-level split is cited, and the missing agent-level control is stated in Limitations.
- **A3 (counters): measured.** `w13_counter_dig.py`: the counter gap is real and not a metric artifact. Correlation with hero-WR difference is -0.035; the log-odds and detrended versions give the same gaps. Counters predict real wins (z ~ 12). The counter deficit gives back about a sixth of the timing gain. The net realized win-probability gap is +2.5 to +4.1pp. Counters are now reported in Section 7.
- **A4 (prereg):** `PREREG_FORWARD.md` is rewritten as v2 and not yet registered. The paper's Section 10 now describes an OSF registration, with `\preregdate` / `\prereglink` placeholders.
- **B1-B20:** all applied. The 86% became 91% under clean scoring, and the volume "fifth" was dropped.
- **Still open:** register on OSF (Max); commit the frozen code; decide on ToG blind-review policy; replace the dead anonymous-repo link.
